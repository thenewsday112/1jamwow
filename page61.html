Gareth Southgate, Diego Simeone, Kieran McKenna and Cesc Fabregas are all given the same outside shot of taking the helm as Tuchel.

There are a few more comedic offerings, with Gary Neville listed at 100/1 to take charge – as is sacked former manager Erik ten Hag.

"Honorarkürzungsvorwurf falsch": Krankenkassen werfen Ärzten Termin-Angstmache vor

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Politics  |  Read Source →

Die Gesundheitsreform der Bundesregierung kommt bei den Kassenärzten gar nicht gut an. Wegen der Kürzung von Bonuszahlungen drohen sie mit Terminengpässen. Die Krankenkassen verweisen wiederum auf üppige Praxiseinnahmen.

Im Streit um die Auswirkungen des Gesundheits-Sparpakets auf die Zahl der Arzttermine werfen die gesetzlichen Krankenkassen den Ärzten vor, bei den Versicherten Ängste zu schüren. "Die Behauptung, dass es weniger Arzttermine geben müsse, weil diese schlechter bezahlt würden, ist nicht richtig", sagte die Vize-Chefin des Kassen-Spitzenverbandes, Stefanie Stoff-Ahnis, dem Redaktionsnetzwerk Deutschland (RND). "Das ist ein Affront gegenüber 75 Millionen gesetzlich Versicherten und deren Arbeitgebern, die über ihre Krankenkassenbeiträge die Honorare der niedergelassenen Ärztinnen und Ärzte finanzieren", betonte sie und kritisierte: "Damit werden Menschen verunsichert, die auf medizinische Hilfe angewiesen sind."

Kassenärzte-Chef Andreas Gassen hatte davor gewarnt, dass gesetzlich Versicherte wegen der Kürzung von Mitteln für die ambulante Versorgung künftig deutlich länger auf einen Arzttermin warten müssten. Mit ihrer Reform der ambulanten Versorgung habe die Bundesregierung gerade mehr als 50 Millionen Termine gestrichen. Die Terminnot werde sich daher verschärfen.

Stoff-Ahnis betonte hingegen, mit dem Sparpaket würden nicht die Vergütungen für die ärztlichen Leistungen gesenkt, sondern Zuschläge und Bonuszahlungen für eine besonders schnelle Terminvergabe gestrichen. Denn das Ziel der Politik, durch diese Extrahonorare die Terminwartezeiten zu verkürzen, sei nicht erreicht worden. Im Gegenteil seien die Wartezeiten sogar länger geworden. "Es war und ist nicht zu rechtfertigen, Beitragsgelder für wirkungslose Extrahonorare auszugeben", sagte Stoff-Ahnis.

Stoff-Ahnis verwies auch auf das Ergebnis der Honorarverhandlungen für 2027. Danach steigt die Vergütung für ärztliche Leistungen um 2,2 Prozent oder eine Milliarde Euro. Unter Berücksichtigung der gestrichenen Extrazahlungen würden die gesetzlich Versicherten über ihre Krankenkassenbeiträge an die niedergelassenen Ärztinnen und Ärzte weiterhin Honorare in einer Größenordnung von rund 50 Milliarden Euro zahlen. Sie erinnerte zudem daran, dass die Mediziner aus ihren Praxen im Durchschnitt pro Jahr einen Reinertrag (vor Steuern und Sozialversicherungen) von deutlich über 200.000 Euro erhielten: "Und es gibt keinen Anlass zu glauben, dass sich daran etwas ändern wird."

JOJ Dakar 2026: en RDC, Mervie Yemebele, un jeune talent de l'épée congolais [1/5]

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

En République démocratique du Congo, Mervie Yembele, 16 ans, est l’un des neuf athlètes congolais sélectionnés pour participer aux Jeux olympiques de la jeunesse de Dakar prévus du 31 octobre au 13 novembre. Sa discipline : l’escrime qu'il pratique au quotidien à Kinshasa.

Les lames des escrimeurs s’entrechoquent dans la commune de Lemba, à Kinshasa. Sur un terrain de sport en plein air, une poignée de jeunes, en tenue blanche, manient l’épée ou le fleuret. Parmi eux, Mervie Yembele. « Je m'entraine tous les matins de 8 h à 11 h ou 12 h, du lundi au samedi ; et le soir, mardi, jeudi et samedi », précise-t-il.

Un programme chargé car à 16 ans, le jeune escrimeur se prépare à participer à ses premiers Jeux olympiques de la jeunesse. Son arme de prédilection : le sabre. « La façon de marquer par l'arme et tout, parade et tout. Avec toute cette rapidité dans une tierce de minutes, c'est vraiment chouette », explique-t-il.

Pourtant, il y a quelques années encore, Mervie Yembele ne connaissait pas l’escrime. C’est en regardant les Jeux olympiques de Rio, au Brésil, en 2016, qu’il découvre cette discipline. « Il y a des gens qui se focalisent maintenant à la télé : "Il y a les Jeux olympiques, il y a les Jeux olympiques !" Je voyais des gens porter des tenues et des lames. Je me demandais : c'était quoi en fait ? Et je pensais que c'était seulement à l'extérieur du pays. Et du coup, après un moment, on a contacté mon maître en me disant que "non, ça existe aussi en RDC". Puis, j'ai adhéré à la Fédération », raconte-t-il.

À lire aussiPourquoi les Jeux Olympiques de la Jeunesse ont-ils été créés ?

Visage dissimulé derrière son masque, jambes fléchies en position de garde, le jeune athlète peaufine sa technique. Son objectif : décrocher une médaille aux Jeux olympiques de la jeunesse. « C'est vraiment un honneur pour moi de participer aux JOJ. La RDC ne compte pas de médaille olympique et du coup, aujourd'hui, je crois que c'est ma chance. Je dois saisir cette opportunité et ramener la toute première médaille olympique pour la République démocratique du Congo », affirme-t-il.

Mais pour atteindre ce rêve, encore faut-il disposer des moyens nécessaires. Un défi pour ce jeune sportif congolais, comme l’explique Anaël Konga, vice-président de la Fédération congolaise d’escrime.

« Les conditions d'entraînement sont vraiment médiocres. La fédération n'a pas de salle, nous n'avons pas de sponsors. On n'arrive pas vraiment à former suffisamment d'athlètes pour répondre aux besoins des compétitions. Le rendement sera assez difficile, mais comme le cœur y est. Alors on essaie de faire avec. »

La Fédération congolaise d’escrime compte un peu plus d’une centaine de licenciés.

Tikehau negocia entrar en el capital de MatHolding como socio minoritario

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

La entrada del fondo francés estaría destinada a financiar la compra del gigante israelí Netafim.

La gestora francesa de capital riesgo Tikehau Capital negocia entrar en el accionariado del grupo industrial catalán MatHolding, especializado en tecnologías de riego agrícola y fitosanitarios para la protección de cultivos, como nuevo socio minoritario de la compañía, según varias fuentes del mercado consultadas por EXPANSIÓN.

La operación, de salir adelante, contribuiría a acelerar el crecimiento de la compañía, pues ayudaría a financiar la eventual adquisición de Netafim, empresa israelí líder en sistemas de riego por goteo, según algunas de las fuentes consultadas.

El medio israelí CTech ya avanzó el pasado verano que MatHolding estudiaba adquirir Netafim, cuya valoración podría superar los 1.000 millones de dólares. La empresa española tiene presencia en Israel tras las adquisiciones de las locales Dorot y ARI, posteriormente integradas en Aquestia.

Dado que Netafim es más grande que MatHolding, la publicación israelí también señaló que MatHolding estaba estudiando dar entrada en su capital a un fondo de capital riesgo para poder abordar la compra.

Además de Tikehau, otros fondos de capital riesgo, tanto nacionales como internacionales, se han interesado en la eventual transacción corporativa. AZ Capital trabaja con MatHolding en la estructuración de la deuda asociada a la operación, según las fuentes.

Tikehau, MatHolding y AZ Capital evitaron realizar comentarios al respecto de esta información.

La posible operación se produciría después de que MatHolding se anotara nuevos resultados récord en 2025, con una facturación de 429 millones de euros, que superó en un 6% la contabilizada durante el ejercicio precedente. El ebitda del grupo español durante el año pasado fue de 65 millones de euros, lo que representa un crecimiento del 16% con respecto a 2024.

Estas cifras son las mejores del grupo familiar en sus 90 años de historia. Desde 2019, MatHolding ha crecido a un ritmo medio del 9% anual en facturación y del 17% en ebitda. En la última década, MatHolding ha doblado su cifra de negocio y de cara a los próximos ejercicios pretende hacer lo mismo.

Según las fuentes consultadas, el ebitda actual de la compañía se sitúa en el entorno de los 70 millones de euros.

El accionariado de MatHolding, con sede en Parets del Vallès (Barcelona), está dominado por la familia Matosas. Su presidente, Pau Relat, preside también Fira de Barcelona y siempre ha defendido la continuidad de la empresa en Israel.

Tikehau Capital es una gestora de capital riesgo francesa con 53.500 millones de euros bajo gestión entre todas sus estrategias que ha invertido alrededor de 2.000 millones de euros en el mercado español desde que aterrizó en este mercado en 2017.

A través de su división de private equity controla el grupo de movilidad Eysa, el fabricante de especias Juan Navarro o el grupo de software para el sector energético Isotrol y también participa en la empresa española especializada en inteligencia artificial Multiverse.

Mediante la división de crédito, Tikehau está presente en la deuda de Bollo Natural Fruit, Alvinesa, Legálitas, Pastas Gallo o Angulas Aguinaga, entre otras inversiones del gigante del private equity francés.

Su eventual entrada en el accionariado de MatHolding incrementaría su apuesta por la geografía española.

Назван способ уменьшить нагрев смартфона во время игры

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Для того чтобы уменьшить нагрев смартфона во время игры, пользователям предложили использовать питание от зарядного устройства в обход аккумулятора. Об этом сообщает немецкое издание PC-Welt.

Проверенный способ назвал автор медиа Флориан Кастнер. При обычном подключении к розетке энергия расходуется одновременно на работу телефона и пополнение заряда батареи. Специальный режим позволяет приостановить зарядку аккумулятора и направить питание непосредственно на работу устройства.

По словам Кастнера, это уменьшает дополнительное выделение тепла, связанное с зарядкой батареи. Функция особенно полезна при длительных игровых сессиях, когда аппарат уже нагревается под нагрузкой. При этом она не устраняет нагрев процессора и других компонентов и не гарантирует полного отсутствия перегрева.

Специалист также посоветовал проверить фоновые приложения. Редко используемые программы можно ограничить в настройках батареи, чтобы они реже синхронизировали данные. При этом важные мессенджеры, почту и календарь не стоит полностью лишать фоновой активности — иначе уведомления могут приходить с задержкой.

PAT MCFADDEN: These rogue opportunists MUST be held to account

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

By PAT MCFADDEN, WORK AND PENSIONS SECRETARY

Published: 23:00 GMT, 25 October 2026 | Updated: 23:15 GMT, 25 October 2026

A pensioner recently contacted my department in tears about a benefits claim that had been made on her behalf. She didn't know what it was for and feared it could get her into trouble.

Another had been asked to hand over a large share of her welfare payment to a firm she had used to help her claim it. When she said she couldn't afford the firm's fee, a 'payment plan' was offered and, after being contacted repeatedly by a sales person, she felt pressured to agree to one.

These are not isolated incidents. While the welfare state exists to support people in need, including vulnerable pensioners, it's increasingly being exploited by private business for profit. Both of the women above had made claims for Attendance Allowance, a benefit offered to older people who need help with personal care. And the number of claims for it made by management firms has increased fivefold in the past year alone.

The rapid growth of this industry should concern us all, as these companies charge as much as £1,300 per application, which can eat into any margins enormously.

Some people choose to pay this knowingly, but not everyone. It's clear that many people are not aware that they can access free support from Citizens Advice, Independent Age and a wide network of charities.

But in the coming weeks, the Government will clamp down on claims-management firms by banning them from charging fees. We will introduce a new Code of Practice for the industry, which will be backed, if necessary, by legislation.

While the welfare state exists to support people in need, including vulnerable pensioners, it's increasingly being exploited by private business for profit

This is about more than money. Some claims-management companies promise to 'take care' of the whole process, leaving customers with little understanding of what information has been submitted. We have seen cases where people discovered details had been included on claims – such as exaggerations of their care needs – that they did not know had been submitted.

In the most serious cases, this amounts to fraud. Yet it is often the customer, not the company handling the claim, that is left to worry about the consequences.

Nobody seeking support because of illness, disability or financial difficulty should be put in that position. Alongside the ban is a national awareness campaign, including advertisements in leading newspapers such as the Daily Mail, but also posters in GP surgeries and pharmacies, notifying claimants of the free support available.

It's unacceptable that rogue opportunists are charging people significant fees for support they are entitled to for nothing. It's time to hold them to account.

Belgrade in rapture as Serbian students topple Vucic in historic election

BELGRADE, Oct 25 - Trams and traffic halted by crowds. Tens of thousands of people choking the city's main boulevards; the air thick with smoke; a man on a car waving a flare.

Familiar-sounding scenes after a month of sometimes violent protests across European cities as students take to the streets calling for better education standards and better prospects.

Except, on Sunday night in Serbia's capital Belgrade, students were not protesting but celebrating an election victory after long-time ruler Aleksandar Vucic conceded defeat to a student-backed list of candidates in parliamentary elections.

It was the culmination of a two-year, peaceful protest movement that called for Vucic to step down and an end to what they said is rampant corruption in the Balkan country - a charge Vucic denies.

The students' win marks a profound political shift in the country of some 6 million people and shows what power younger generation have when they are able to harness widespread discontent.

"That is the best thing that happened in my life. We will now breathe freely," said Milica, a law student.

Vucic had ruled as prime minister or president since 2014, and oversaw a period of strong economic growth, but he faced growing opposition from student-led protests after the collapse of a railway station awning killed 16 people in November 2024.

Tomorrow, the disparate group of 250 student-backed candidates will have to turn towards ruling, but Sunday evening was a time to party.

At their headquarters, the unknown candidates, many of them lawyers, professors, actors or farmers, hugged and cheered in near disbelief at what they had achieved.

"The people have won! The people defeated a dictator! The people will lead Serbia!" said Nebojsa Cosic while waving a flag on the street. REUTERS

Published: 23:00 GMT, 25 October 2026 | Updated: 23:09 GMT, 25 October 2026

Andy Burnham called yesterday for ‘an honest discussion’ about the merits of a return to allowing free movement of people between Britain and countries of the European Union.

Perhaps the PM should start by being honest about his own intentions. In June, he specifically told the mainly Eurosceptic voters of Makerfield that he would not re-run the old Brexit arguments.

‘I’m not proposing the UK considers rejoining the EU,’ he said. ‘I respect the decision that was made at the referendum, and it’s going to undermine everything I’ve said about strengthening democracy if we don’t respect that vote.’

It got him elected, but what a difference a few months makes in his morally flexible world. Shamelessly flouting that Makerfield pledge, he is already dragging this country back towards Brussels. He even says we may go ‘all the way’ and apply to rejoin.

Not only has Mr Burnham deceived the people of Makerfield, he is also making a mockery of Labour’s 2024 manifesto, which expressly rejected a return to free movement, the single market or customs union. Hardly the way to start an honest debate.

But then Remainers are used to churning out falsehoods. They love to complain about the notorious Leave campaign bus slogan claiming Britain sent £350,000 a week to the EU. And they had a point. After the UK rebate and other benefits were taken off, it turned out to be merely £252,000 a week. But compared with the mendacity and outrageous scaremongering of the Remain campaign, this was a minor sin.

In June, Andy Burnham told the mainly Eurosceptic voters of Makerfield that he was 'not proposing the UK considers rejoining the EU'

Treasury forecasts before the 2016 vote predicted that Brexit would bring economic catastrophe – a GDP fall of up to 6 per cent, as many as 820,000 more unemployed, a drop in real wages of 3-4 per cent, and house prices plummeting by up to 18 per cent. It was all tosh.

In the event, jobs and vacancies soared, wages and GDP continued to rise, while house prices, far from collapsing, remained buoyant in the years after the referendum.

Spurred into life by Mr Burnham’s double-dealing remarks, Remainers are at it again, spewing out spurious figures purporting to show how disastrous Brexit has been. Yet more humbug.

They don’t mention that going back would mean cancelling the many trade deals made with other countries since Brexit and relinquishing control of our trade policy.

Or that it could mean state investment in steel and other domestic industries becoming illegal and that AI and other technical developments could be hampered by EU regulation.

Nor the net £9.2billion a year we no longer pay in membership fees. As our rebate would not be renewed, that figure would be substantially higher if we went back in. Would we be allowed back anyway, and if so, would we have to join the single currency? Would there be a fresh entry fee, would we have to join the border-free Schengen zone?

If Mr Burnham really wants to rake over dead coals, he must take account of the demonstrable cons of shackling ourselves once more to the ailing EU, as well as the imagined pros.

Vanishingly few people want to go through the Brexit psychodrama again – including another toxic debate over free movement. It can only bring more rancour and division.

Four months ago Mr Burnham claimed he respected the referendum result, while clearly plotting to overturn it all along. Is that his idea of honesty?
