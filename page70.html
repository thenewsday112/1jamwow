Shapiro looks like a 2028 powerhouse — with an asterisk

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro is an electoral juggernaut in America's biggest swing state — and he's widely seen as a likely contender for president in 2028. But strategists in both parties question how tested he really is.

  • Shapiro's success — he's cruised to victory in multiple statewide races — has come with an asterisk: He hasn't faced a well-financed opponent in recent contests, leading some political insiders to see him as more of a wildcard than his resume suggests.

Why it matters: Shapiro's unfamiliarity with intense, bruising campaigns could become a liability in the 2028 Democratic primary, where if he was a top candidate, he'd be confronted with a barrage of attack ads, opposition research and rivals with deep pockets.

  • A plugged-in Democratic operative in Pennsylvania suggested that because of Shapiro's lack of being tested, "it is totally possible that he has an incredible glass jaw — and that he is the Scott Walker of 2028."
  • Walker was the swing-state Wisconsin governor who looked good on paper as a potential presidential candidate but flopped after entering the 2016 GOP primary.

Zoom in: In next week's election, Shapiro is running for a second four-year term as the Keystone State's governor. He's on pace to defeat Republican challenger Stacy Garrity by double digits, according to the latest polls.

  • The money gap is gargantuan: Shapiro has dropped $42 million on campaign ads, according to the ad-tracking firm AdImpact.

Garrity, the state's treasurer, has spent just $3 million on ads — a drop in the ocean in a state as large as Pennsylvania, with its expensive advertising markets.

  • It's not that Garrity prefers, say, door-knocking to TV ads. She's just outmatched: Campaign finance reports show she's only raised about $11 million this cycle, compared with Shapiro's $64 million.
  • Making matters worse for Garrity, the Republican Governors Association (RGA) has not come to her rescue on the airwaves.

Christopher Nicholas, a longtime GOP strategist in Pennsylvania, said the dynamic has "allowed Shapiro to skate with essentially having a one-sided campaign."

  • "A lot of people think he has a glass chin because he's never really had a million dollars of negative TV shoved down his throat in a week," Nicholas said.

Zoom out: Shapiro caught a break when he first ran for governor in 2022 against Republican Doug Mastriano, a far-right state senator widely viewed as a historically weak candidate disliked even by the GOP establishment.

  • Mastriano, who was strapped for cash, spent just $2 million on ads that year. Shapiro and his allies spent $55 million.
  • A conservative outside group invested $8 million on advertising in the election but abandoned the race that September after Shapiro embraced school vouchers, a priority for many on the right. The RGA stayed out of the race.

In both of his gubernatorial contests, Shapiro has run unopposed in the Democratic primaries, leaving him little experience of intra-party electoral battles.

  • It was a similar story when Shapiro twice was elected as Pennsylvania's attorney general. He vastly outspent his Republican opponents.

What they're saying: Shapiro's allies argue that he's had token opposition precisely because he's a formidable candidate — that more talented Republicans don't want to waste their time and money running against such a hard-to-beat opponent with big fundraising chops. The governor has also sometimes cut off his GOP rivals' money spigots by courting Republican donors.

  • "Sun Tzu was always about picking the battle you want to fight," said Doc Sweitzer, a Pennsylvania Democratic ad maker. "Josh has an incredible skill of picking the opponents."
  • Asked for comment, Shapiro spokesperson Manuel Bonder said: "Winning elections in Pennsylvania and putting a check on Donald Trump is the only thing we're focused on — and believe it or not, it's harder than sitting at home giving unnamed quotes to Axios."
  • Garrity spokesperson Matt Beynon responded: "No amount of money will heal the wounds Josh Shapiro's inflicted over the past four years, it's going to take new leadership."

The other side: Shapiro is far from the only potential Democratic presidential candidate who hasn't squared off against a top-notch statewide Republican candidate.

  • Possible White House hopefuls from deep-blue territory — such as New York Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, or with little experience defeating Republicans statewide, like former Transportation Secretary Pete Buttigieg, have either never run in or never won remotely competitive general elections.

Flashback: Shapiro's critics make the case that regardless of why he hasn't been challenged much, he and his team haven't had to deal with reams of opposition research being dug up about him, or tens of millions of dollars in negative ads aired against him.

  • One of the only times Shapiro came close to experiencing that was when he was under consideration to be former Vice President Kamala Harris' running mate in 2024 — and, they say, that didn't turn out well.
  • Shapiro faced a mountain of tough press over topics on which he'd never faced significant scrutiny, such as a college op-ed piece in which he called Palestinians "too battle-minded" to exist peacefully with Israel.
  • At the time, even some of Shapiro's closest supporters felt he didn't respond effectively to the negative articles.

Zwei mickrige Pünktchen fehlen: Antonelli hat nach USA-Sieg schon eine Hand am historischen WM-Pokal

Die WM-Entscheidung ist vertagt - doch Kimi Antonelli dem historischen Titelgewinn in der Formel 1 näher denn je. Der Italiener hat den Großen Preis der USA gewonnen und seinen Vorsprung in der Weltmeisterschaft weiter ausgebaut.

Die in Austin/Texas mögliche vorzeitige Krönung zum jüngsten Champion in der Geschichte der Königsklasse blieb aus. Lewis Hamilton im Ferrari als Vierter und Mercedes-Teamkollege George Russell als Achter sammelten die nötigen Punkte, um zumindest theoretisch noch in den Titelkampf einzugreifen.

Antonellis neunten Saisonsieg verhinderten sie nicht. Der 20-Jährige gewann von der Pole und verwies Charles Leclerc im Ferrari und Red-Bull-Star Max Verstappen auf die Plätze. Das Hinterbänkler-Team Cadillac verpasste beim Heimspiel mit seinen Piloten Sergio Perez und Valtteri Bottas wie erwartet die ersten Zähler der Saison, Bottas schied spät mit einem Defekt aus.

Antonellis WM-Party ist aber nur noch Formsache: Fünf Rennen vor dem Saisonende hat er 365 Punkte auf dem Konto und 123 Zähler Vorsprung auf Russell, Hamilton als Dritter liegt 124 Punkte zurück. 125 Punkte sind noch zu vergeben. Schon am kommenden Sonntag in Mexiko-Stadt dürfte das Titelrennen entschieden werden.

Die Basis für ein erfolgreiches Rennen hatte Antonelli mit einer unerwarteten Pole-Runde im Qualifying gelegt. Auf den Punkt genau hatte der Youngster die anhaltenden Probleme mit überhitzenden Reifen in den Griff bekommen und sich so die ideale Ausgangslage für den WM-Matchball verschafft. "Ich habe gut geschlafen, es ist nur ein weiteres Rennen, auch wenn der Kontext ein wenig anders ist", sagte Antonelli vor dem Start bei Sky und gab zu, doch ein wenig nervös zu sein: "Es würde mir viel bedeuten, das Wochenende gut abzuschließen."

Die erste Aufgabe, die Führung am Ende der steilen Start- und Zielgeraden zu verteidigen, gelang Antonelli mit Bravour. Antonelli erwischte einen Traumstart und ließ Verstappen auf dem zweiten Startplatz keine Chance für einen Angriff. In den ersten Runden hielt der Youngster den viermaligen Weltmeister problemlos auf Abstand. Einen Vorteil im Fernduell mit Russell erhielt Antonelli durch eine Fünf-Sekunden-Strafe für den Briten. Russell hatte in der ersten Runde im Duell mit Oscar Piastri (McLaren) die Strecke verlassen und dadurch einen unerlaubten Vorteil erlangt.

Antonelli war mit Medium-Reifen gestartet, Mercedes reagierte auf die frühen Boxenstopps der Rivalen nicht und setzte anders als die Konkurrenz auf eine Ein-Stopp-Strategie - es sollte die klügere Wahl sein. Verstappen machte auf frischen Softs Druck, vorn hielt Antonelli die Rundenzeiten konstant.

In der 25. Runde wechselte Antonelli auf die harte Mischung und nahm Kurs auf den Sieg. Verstappen schimpfte derweil über Probleme an seinem "unfahrbaren" Boliden. Antonelli hatte nach Verstappens zweitem Stopp die Aufgabe, seine Reifen zu managen und an der Spitze die Verfolger auf Abstand zu halten. Das gelang dem designierten Champion mühelos.

La lista Estudiantil gana las elecciones al expresidente Aleksandar Vucic, que lleva 12 años al frente de Serbia, país al que ha encaminado por la senda del autoritarismo, según ha reconocido el expresidente en una intervención televisiva minutos después del cierre de las urnas.

De las calles a las urnas: un movimiento estudiantil está a punto de tomar el poder y derrotar al líder ultra de Serbia

“No tenemos los resultados. Mucha gente se llevará una sorpresa y se darán cuenta de hasta qué punto han mentido algunos. Basándonos en una muestra estratificada, está claro que hemos perdido estas elecciones», ha señalado Vučić.

Según la primera proyección de Ipsos/CeSID, la Lista Estudiantil arrasa al actual líder serbio, alcanzando el 52%, mientras que la lista de Vučić se queda en apenas un 39,3%. Con estos datos, se estima que obtendrán 138 escaños en el nuevo Parlamento —superando con creces los 126 necesarios para la mayoría absoluta—, frente a los 104 de Vučić. A las 19 horas, la participación rozaba el 68%, 13 puntos más que en las elecciones anteriores. Esta subida daba una clara pista del nivel de movilización contra el líder nacionalista.

“La Lista Estudiantil ha obtenido una victoria decisiva y una mayoría absoluta en la Asamblea Nacional de Serbia. Tras dos años de movilización sostenida —en lo que podría describirse como las condiciones electorales más difíciles en la historia multipartidista de Serbia—, los estudiantes han transformado la extendida demanda social de cambio en una clara mayoría parlamentaria. La mayor participación electoral registrada hasta la fecha demuestra la determinación de la ciudadanía por definir el futuro de su país”, señala a elDiario.es el analista Nemanja Štiplija, fundador del think tank Centro de Política Contemporánea y editor del portal European Western Balkans.

“El impulso político observado en Hungría se ha trasladado a Serbia. Las elecciones se convirtieron, en la práctica, en un referéndum en el que Aleksandar Vucic y la Lista Estudiantil se perfilaron como las dos únicas alternativas creíbles. Los votantes se enfrentaron a una elección clara: la continuidad del gobierno actual o el cambio político que representan los estudiantes. Todas las demás listas quedaron por debajo del umbral electoral, lo que pone de manifiesto hasta qué punto esta polarización determinó el resultado”, explica Štiplija. “Este desenlace otorga a la Lista Estudiantil un mandato sólido que conlleva una gran responsabilidad. La confianza ciudadana debe traducirse ahora en instituciones independientes, un gobierno que rinda cuentas y unas condiciones en las que, en futuras elecciones, ejercer los derechos democráticos fundamentales deje de ser una lucha”, añade.

De hecho, el primer ministro húngaro, Péter Magyar, ha sido uno de los primeros en felicitar a los ganadores. “Me alegró mucho saber cuánta fuerza les había dado nuestra victoria de abril durante esta difícil lucha”, ha afirmado.

“Esta noche se trata de algo más que de los resultados: se trata de las personas que siguieron creyendo cuando las cosas estaban más difíciles, que se negaron a rendirse, a aceptar la idea de que nada podría cambiar jamás”, ha afirmado Radomir Saičić, profesor de química y uno de los candidatos. “Esta noche hemos demostrado que el cambio no solo es posible, sino que existe la voluntad para llevarlo a cabo. Ahora debemos hacernos merecedores de la confianza que se nos ha otorgado”.

El movimiento estudiantil lleva dos años protestando de manera interrumpida en las calles de Serbia y ha puesto en marcha un novedoso experimento democrático. Incapaz de aplastar a los estudiantes por la fuerza, la división o el menosprecio, Vučić se arriesgó en septiembre anunciando su dimisión como presidente y convocando elecciones anticipadas, con la aparente esperanza de pillar a los estudiantes desprevenidos, pero estos respondieron rápidamente.

Los 250 candidatos fueron en su mayoría propuestos por las facultades universitarias (entre ellos hay más de 160 doctores), con algunos agricultores y profesionales de otros ámbitos, y cada candidato tuvo que ser aprobado en las sesiones plenarias de las protestas estudiantiles. El programa político también ha sido decidido por el pleno, con el resultado de que se han minimizado los temas que generan polémica, especialmente la política exterior: la cuestión de la futura adhesión a la UE se ha dejado en manos de un referéndum y se afirma que Kosovo es parte integrante de Serbia.

Vucic, el exministro ultra de Milosevic que se juega la reelección ante un movimiento estudiantil, tras 12 años liderando Serbia

Vucic, por su parte, fue ministro de Información de Slobodan Milosevic, que falleció encarcelado en La Haya, donde estaba siendo juzgado por crímenes de guerra, de lesa humanidad y genocidio. El dirigente ha sido capaz de moverse entre un áspero nacionalismo panserbio, ortodoxo y antioccidental y las maneras más aterciopeladas del europeísmo reformista y democratizador, sin renunciar a ninguno de los dos polos en función de la coyuntura.

As is tradition, iFixit has torn open the latest iPhone and laid its parts bare on a table. It found some interesting details about how Apple might be hiding the crease on the Duo’s folding screen and gave it a 5/10 repairability score. That might not seem particularly high, but it’s the best score the site has given to a foldable yet.

The internal screen’s matte nano-texture cover doesn’t just make it more readable in bright sunlight; it also helps mask the crease by scattering reflections. When looking at the display under a microscope, iFixit also found oval-shaped subpixels arranged at various angles, which the site believes are designed to reduce artifacts and further mask the crease down the middle of the display.

The nano-texture outer layer is relatively easy to replace without swapping out the OLED underneath. If you do need to repair the display itself, however, good luck. As is the case with other foldables, it’s an expensive nightmare. Dust did make it into the USB-C port, speaker grills, and the hinge — making it a bit crunchy. But noticeably less of the glow dust found its way inside the phone than the Z Fold8 or Pixel 11 Pro Fold.

As for reports of damaged screens and cracking hinges, iFixit has some theories. The hinge on the iPhone Duo is incredibly complex, and they suspect part of it sometimes isn’t descending properly. There is also an accordion cable that passes over that exact spot, and it’s near the edge of the battery. It’s possible this is concentrating pressure and leading to the “audible crack” reported by some and the broken screens. Apple did not immediately return a request for comment.

Pedro Pasquín, jefe de Lazard en España, se suma al 'boom' del capital riesgo

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Ya hay más de 1.500 entidades y fondos de 'private equity' registrados en la CNMV, por el aluvión de grandes fortunas hacia el sector.

El auge del private equity en España tiene un nuevo protagonista tras la creación de una sociedad de capital riesgo (SCR) por parte de Pedro Pasquín, el vicepresidente de Lazard en España y socio director de la oficina de este banco de negocios en el país desde su apertura en 1999.

Pasquín ha creado junto a otros miembros de su familia la entidad Bermeo Partners, registrada en la Comisión Nacional del Mercado de Valores (CNMV) con un capital inicial de 1,21 millones de euros. La gestión de la SCR ha sido encomendada a Renta 4.

El objetivo de la entidad es invertir en empresas y activos no cotizados, principalmente a través de otras sociedades de capital riesgo.

El responsable de Lazard en España se une a otros directivos financieros que han creado SCR familiares, caso de Olaf Díaz-Pintado, ex consejero delegado de Goldman Sachs en España; de Álvaro de Remedios, presidente de Arcano Parterns; y de Fernando Ortiz, socio fundador de ProA Capital. Además, múltiples empresarios, como las familias Puig, Arrufat y Daurella; y altos ejecutivos como Ignacio Sánchez-Galán, presidente de Iberdrola; han constituido vehículos de este tipo.

Al final de 2025, según la CNMV, había 1.501 sociedades y fondos de capital riesgo en España, cifra que duplica la de hace tres años.

Este boom se debe al interés de las fortunas españolas por diversificar más allá de las acciones y los bonos cotizados; y, por otro lado, a las ventajas fiscales, ya que los dueños de más del 5% de una SCR pueden acogerse a los beneficios tributarios de las empresas familiares.

En el caso de Pasquín, no se conoce el importe de su patrimonio, y Lazard Asesores Financieros evita desvelar en sus cuentas anuales los sueldos de sus máximos directivos. Esto hace que Deloitte, en sus auditorías de los últimos dos años, haya recogido una salvedad en su informe: "los administradores de la Sociedad no han incluido en la memoria de las cuentas anuales adjuntas a 31 de diciembre de 2025, tal y como se exige por el artículo 260 del Texto Refundido de la Ley de Sociedades de Capital, el importe de sueldos, dietas y remuneraciones devengadas en el ejercicio 2025, ni el importe de los créditos o anticipos concedidos u obligaciones en materia de pensiones y seguros de vida contraídas al cierre del ejercicio, en relación con los miembros actuales o anteriores del consejo de administración y de la alta dirección".

What have Man Utd changed to turn around WSL form?

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Man City's 100% record ended with Manchester derby draw

Derby day underlined the changing picture at Manchester United, with Eva Olid's team proving they are made of stern stuff by fighting back for a point against rivals Manchester City.

This was the strongest performance yet under new head coach Olid, who after a slow start is seeing signs of real progress, exemplified in a hard-earned 1-1 draw on Sunday.

United not only ended City's 100% start to the Women's Super League season, but they have also extended their own unbeaten streak to eight matches in all competitions.

It is a remarkable turnaround considering United were without a win and low on confidence after their opening three WSL games.

Their disappointing start to the season was epitomised by a 5-0 drubbing at Chelsea's hands, but "everything" about the performance against City pleased Olid.

"I think it was one of our best performances in general, in terms of the fight and hard work and how we were pressing," Olid said.

"How this team has built in confidence from day one to now, in a short period of time, has been massive. It is still a process, but today was a huge performance."

United now sit fourth in the table after seven matches.

The most noticeable changes have happened on the pitch as Olid, who joined in the summer, conceded her side needed to revert to old ways.

They were struggling, initially, to adapt to her new style which demands lots of possession, an attacking mindset and high-risk football.

United toiled in defeats by London City Lionesses and Chelsea, having just two shots on target and less than 40% possession in both matches.

Olid felt her team were trying to do too much and had lost sight of some of their strengths, including their defensive organisation.

She urged her players to revert to what they knew before their 1-1 draw with Arsenal, which showed signs of improvement.

"The things they were good at before, they need to keep and at the same time, little by little, we need to implement our new style," Olid said before that game.

"It's trying to find this balance. Maybe they thought I would expect them to do everything in the first match but that is impossible. You need time."

Manchester United failed to win any of their first three WSL matches

That draw at Emirates Stadium was the catalyst for change at United, offering a foundation to build upon.

They had a tough start but then had a run of games against lower-ranked opposition, and two successive WSL matches at home.

They won the maximum nine points, scored six goals, conceded just two and almost doubled their possession percentage.

By returning to a more familiar defensive structure and adding in elements of Olid's new style, they began to cause more problems up front too.

Having had just 197 passes ending in the final third in their first three games, they recorded a combined 529 against West Ham, Liverpool and Charlton Athletic.

They quadrupled the number of shots they took too, going from 10 to 42 and won more duels in the air.

That helped to build confidence going into Sunday's derby at Leigh Sports Village, and when Elisabeth Terland fired in a late United equaliser it did not feel like a shock.

Perhaps the biggest changes at United have been off the pitch.

Olid explained that her side had navigated a "transitional period" and had taken part in big discussions following their five-goal home humbling by Chelsea.

Players needed to find confidence and Olid said talks had to bring a solution.

"It has been a lot of hard work to change that, and a lot of honest conversations about what we could do and how we could connect," said Olid on Sunday.

"Sometimes you need a difficult moment to react and see where the problems were [that] we had to solve as a team. It had to happen, to be the team we are now."

The team's form at the start of the season had an impact on individuals.

United vice-captain Ella Toone missed out on England selection for the first time under Sarina Wiegman - albeit she was later summoned as a squad reinforcement.

Toone responded at club level by scoring in United's 3-1 win at Charlton in mid October.

Summer signing Andrea Medina has grown to become a crucial member of the United squad, and the international break proved to be a valuable time for Olid to adjust to her new role, reflect on what needed to change and to spend time on the training pitch.

England defender and United captain Maya le Tissier was among those to return with renewed energy having impressed for her country during the break.

The backroom staff positions were also confirmed and Olid has been able to build a group she hopes can lead United to success going forward.

Fellow Spaniard Jose Herrera joined as assistant coach, alongside Gavin Beith, who worked with Olid at former club Hearts.

Speaking about the international break, Olid said: "I had time to re-organise everything, the roles for the new staff and how we are going to work together.

"This break, for me, was amazing. It was like a new start. It was about keeping the good energy from the last matches but then having a new start."

Now they have proven they can compete with the WSL's best, Olid hopes United can push on and build on their draw with City.

"We need to have our feet on the floor. This season is long and with really good teams. We need to keep improving and learning," Olid said on Sunday.

"If we have the desire and fight we had today, not a lot of things can stop us. We still need to keep improving, but today I could see moments."
