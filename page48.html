A collision between a car and a ute outside North Canberra Hospital led to a brief closure of a section of Haydon Drive in Bruce on Monday morning.

Зарплаты россиян вырастут до конца года

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Фото: Maksim Konstantinov / Global Look Press

Среднемесячная начисленная зарплата россиян до удержания НДФЛ по итогам 2026 года достигнет от 114 тысяч до 120 тысяч рублей. Об этом сообщил «Газете.Ru» кандидат экономических наук, доцент Финансового университета при правительстве РФ Игорь Балынин.

Он обратил внимание, что в июле показатель вырос до 109 524 рублей (в сравнении с 99 305 рублями в 2025 году). Разница составила 10 219 рублей — около 10,3 процента.

«Одним из факторов роста является повышение минимального размера оплаты труда. В 2020 году МРОТ составлял 12 130 рублей, в 2025-м — 22 440 рублей, а в 2026-м — 27 093 рубля. Таким образом, за последний год он увеличился на 4653 рубля», — напомнил экономист.

Еще одним определяющим фактором является конкуренция компаний за работников, указал Балынин. Из-за нехватки специалистов работодателям приходится повышать привлекательность предложений как для новых, так и для действующих сотрудников. Особенно заметен спрос на квалифицированные кадры, нужные для технологического развития предприятий.

Специалист уточнил, что рост среднего показателя зарплат не значит, что каждого работника ждет одинаковая прибавка — величина обобщенная, на нее влияют изменения оплаты и структуры занятости.

До этого в Центробанке РФ заявляли, что рост зарплат в стране замедляется и постепенно сближается с ростом производительности труда.

New York Giants head coach John Harbaugh and quarterback J.J. McCarthy broke down their overtime victory over the Houston Texans.

Cam Skattebo has lowered his shoulder numerous times in his early NFL career, delivering bone-rattling thuds into opponents to help his New York Giants.

In Sunday’s win over the Houston Texans, though, Skattebo was flagged for unnecessary roughness after officials deemed he crossed a line when he laid the boom on safety M.J. Stewart, sending him to the turf after a big gain on the ground.

Skattebo was livid at the call, as was head coach John Harbaugh , who had to hold his running back behind him on the sideline before displaying his own displeasure at the flag being thrown.

Brazil President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva was elected to a fourth, nonconsecutive term in the nation's presidential election, according to early results released Sunday evening.

Why it matters: Lula's victory deals a setback to Brazil's right-wing populist movement and preserves a major counterweight to President Trump's influence in Latin America, with tensions over trade and tariffs threatening to persist.

  • Lula, the incumbent candidate, was facing Flávio Bolsonaro, the son of former Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro. Flávio was widely seen as the heir to the nation's right-wing populist movement.

The latest: Lula thanked the Brazilian people after his election win, but Bolsonaro told reporters he would not accept the results and said they should be annulled, according to translations.

The big picture: The election has been shaped by the impact of U.S. tariffs, climate change and the Amazon rainforest, 60% of which is in Brazil.

  • Bolsonaro campaigned on public security and tackling corruption, presenting himself as a continuation of his father's political legacy, while Lula emphasized defending Brazilian sovereignty against U.S. pressure.
  • Yet substantive policy debate was limited, with both candidates focusing instead on corruption allegations levied against the opposing camp, wrote Matthew Taylor, a Brazil politics expert at American University.

Zoom in: After the U.S. imposed additional 25% tariffs on some Brazilian goods this summer, Lula accused Bolsonaro of being "Trump's candidate" and the U.S. president of wanting to "colonize" Brazil.

  • Lula has enacted climate policies aimed at reversing environmental deregulation under the previous administration, while Bolsonaro denied climate change and pledged to continue his father's political legacy.
  • Amazon deforestation has fallen 71% since 2022, the year before Lula returned to office, reaching its lowest annual level on record in 2026. Yet Lula's environmental record is complicated by his support for expanded oil development.
  • Lula defeated Jair Bolsonaro in the previous election. The elder Bolsonaro is now serving a 27-year prison sentence for plotting a coup to overturn the 2022 election, though he remains under house arrest due to health problems.

Two storms slam northern Europe, cut power to 150,000 homes

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Storms Boelo and Austen kill a man in Scotland and cut power to thousands of homes in the UK, France and Germany.

A pair of storms battering northern Europe have killed one man, cut power to more than 150,000 homes, and disrupted flights and phone networks.

More than 82,000 homes in the United Kingdom, nearly 60,000 in France, and at least 10,000 in Germany had no electricity on Sunday after storms Boelo and Austen passed through the region within 24 hours, according to authorities in the countries.

A falling tree killed a man, 60, in southwestern Scotland on Sunday, while another falling tree injured two women in central Scotland, the Press Association reported.

Winds of up to 135 km/h (84 mph) were recorded in the Argyll and Bute regions of western Scotland. Trees and power cables brought down by the storm blocked roads and rail lines across Scotland and northern England.

In Brighton, on England’s southern coast, Austen blew away an unused pier on Friday.

Authorities have warned of flood risk in regions of both Scotland and England.

The Met Office, the UK’s national forecaster, warned that Boelo would bring a danger to life from flying debris and crashing waves and said people should stay indoors as much as possible.

Across the English Channel, Europe battled its first big storm of the autumn, with strong winds inflicting widespread power cuts.

French energy company Enedis said that at Austen’s peak on Saturday, some 288,000 homes had no electricity. Some 59,000 remained without power at midday Sunday, mainly in Normandy and Brittany.

A marathon runner in the midst of a race in northern France was lightly injured by a tree knocked over in strong winds.

In Germany, Austen cut power to 10,000 homes in and around the southwestern town of Freiburg-Breisgau on Sunday. The town’s main hospital said it had to limit service to emergency cases because of the blackout, which also cut the mobile phone network.

German police said two people were seriously hurt on Saturday by scaffolding blown away by Austen’s strong winds at a market in the town of Nastatten.

The storms also hit the Netherlands and Belgium, where falling trees injured four people in Brussels on Saturday.

In the Netherlands, north of the island of Schiermonnikoog, three crew members on board a freight ship were injured in the storms, according to the country’s coastguard. Two of them were winched off and taken by helicopter to a hospital on the mainland.

The storms caused hundreds of flights to be cancelled or diverted across northern Europe, including at Amsterdam’s Schiphol airport, one of the continent’s busiest hubs.

Experts have warned of more extreme weather events due to climate change.

Действующий президент Бразилии Луис Инасиу Лула да Силва переизбран на четвертый срок. По данным Высшего избирательного суда страны, после обработки более 99,67 процента бюллетеней он получил 51,76 процента голосов, а его соперник, сенатор Флавиу Болсонару, — 48,24 процента.

Согласно официальному календарю TSE, новый срок Лулы начнется 5 января 2027 года.

Это первый случай в Бразилии, когда кандидат, уступивший в первом туре, выиграл второй. В первом туре Болсонару набрал 47,03 процента голосов против 45,16 процента у Лулы.

Лула родился в 1945 году в бедной семье на северо-востоке Бразилии. В юности он работал чистильщиком обуви, а затем токарем на металлургическом заводе, где в результате производственной аварии потерял палец.

В 1970-х годах он стал одним из лидеров профсоюзного движения металлургов и организатором крупных забастовок в промышленном регионе Сан-Паулу. В 1980 году — одним из основателей Партии трудящихся (PT), а после трех неудачных попыток выиграл президентские выборы в 2002 году и руководил страной в 2003–2010 годах.

Депутат Конгресса Бразилии от правящей Партии трудящихся Реймонт Отони в беседе с ТАСС заявил, что победа Лулы да Силвы вне всяких сомнений приведет к безусловному укреплению связей южноамериканской республики с Москвой.

Председатель правительства РФ Михаил Мишустин и президент Бразилии Луис Инасиу Лула да Силва во время встречи во дворце Итамарати в Бразилиа 5 февраля 2026 года

Парламентарий подчеркнул, что новый мандат политика придаст импульс развитию в латиноамериканском и европейском векторах, а также взаимодействию в рамках сообщества БРИКС с Россией, Китаем, Индией и ЮАР.

Это безусловное усиление наших связей, в этом нет никаких сомнений

В свою очередь, профессор политологии Бразильского федерального университета Рио-Гранди-ду-Сул Фабиано Мельничук уточнял, что поставки удобрений и топлива из России в Бразилию, играющие ключевую роль в торговле между странами, не прекратятся после президентских выборов вне зависимости от их исхода.

Эти поставки жизненно важны для бразильского агробизнеса, а на его поддержку как раз и опирается [кандидат от правой Либеральной партии Флавиу] Болсонару

Политолог полагал, что разрыв подобных экономических связей маловероятен — причем как в случае переизбрания действующего президента Лулы да Силвы, так и при смене администрации. Однако в более чувствительных сферах, например в ядерной энергетике, при победе Болсонару изменения не исключены, если США станут давить на новое руководство Бразилии.

В прошлом месяце Бразилия нарастила импорт российской рыбы до исторического максимума за все время двусторонней торговли. В сентябре объем поставок составил 851,2 тысячи долларов, а по итогам трех кварталов импорт достиг 3,5 миллиона долларов.

Основу закупок составляет приготовленная рыба, в частности сушеная треска; кроме того, были небольшие партии замороженной рыбы, рыбного мяса и филе.

Кроме того, после снятия запрета на экспорт Россия возобновила поставки в страну дизельного топлива. Танкер Leruo, вышедший 16 октября из российского балтийского порта ‌Приморск, направился в бразильский Манаус — он перевозил 300 тысяч баррелей сырья.

В этом году Бразилия получила около 24 процентов российского дизтоплива.

'Day Zero' where UK reservoirs run dry could happen before Christmas, ministers are secretly warned

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Published: 01:59 GMT, 26 October 2026 | Updated: 03:12 GMT, 26 October 2026

Ministers have been secretly warned that reservoirs could run dry within weeks, causing chaos for families in the run-up to Christmas.

The Government's emergency committee, Cobra, is meeting twice a week for crisis talks about water provision in the south-west of England and Sussex.

A 'reasonable worst-case scenario' drawn up in secret by officials suggested water stocks could plummet to zero per cent in weeks.

Without action, it could lead to taps running dry in homes and affect the construction of a nuclear power plant.

Wimbleball reservoir which supplies more than 400,000 people in Somerset is currently at 13 per cent capacity, while Ardingly, which serves 280,000 customers in West Sussex, is at just 17 per cent.

A summer of record-breaking heatwaves led to widespread droughts, with some parts of England still under hosepipe bans even after two autumn storms have hit the country.

Sussex and the south-west were two of the areas worst affected by the lack of rainfall, having faced their driest April to September for more than a century and are still facing critically low water supplies.

Crisis plans have been drawn up for a terrifying 'Day Zero' scenario where reservoirs run completely dry in each area.

Pictured: Wimbleball reservoir - which is at 14 per cent capacity

Ardingly in West Sussex, which supplies about 95,000 properties served by South East Water in Haywards Heath, Uckfield and the surrounding areas, is at 19 per cent capacity

Fears of winter droughts have led to requests from water companies for the government to impose non-essential use bans which would prevent businesses from being able to operate car washes, water sports fields and refill private pools.

Southern Water, South East Water and South West Water have all called on Environment Secretary Dame Angela Eagle to consider such bans.

South West Water has told the government privately it does not anticipate Wimbleball running dry, due to preventative measures it had put in place, while South East Water does not expect to run dry until next year, if situations don't improve.

However, concerns still remain for provisions in Devon's Venford reservoir and 'day zero' risk monitoring has begun for Stithians and Drift reservoirs in Cornwall.

Even if the reservoirs did run completely dry, water companies would likely be able to transfer supplies from elsewhere.

Despite wet weather returning across the country, experts warned that normal levels of rainfall will not be enough to counteract the effects of the drought.

Officials said rainfall would need to be 120 per cent higher than average in the next six months in order to provide a nationwide recovery.

Water companies have been told to conduct daily monitoring of reservoir and river levels and tougher water quality monitoring, and the Environment Agency will increase compliance checks on businesses that abstract water to check they are only taking permitted amounts.

Last week Dame Angela ordered the Environment Agency to prioritise public water supply and allow fewer releases of reservoir water into rivers to help clean them and keep ecosystems healthy to reduce 'supply interruption'.

However, this has led to fears about the effect such a move could have on salmon and other fish, which could perish due to a lack of clean water.

The government told The Times: 'Customer water supplies remain uninterrupted. We have instructed water companies to transfer water to support the lowest reservoirs, speed up leak detection and deliver daily monitoring of reservoir and river levels to help ease the pressure on public supplies.'

South West Water said it was not expecting any water restrictions in early November. It said: 'We are taking urgent action to protect drinking water supplies, repairing around 440 leaks a week and transferring three million litres of water a day to areas where it is needed most.

'We are also exploring additional water sources and working directly with households and high-using businesses to reduce consumption.'

South East Water said 'day zero' was not being considered for 2027 given the actions it had taken. Southern Water and EDF Energy, the company behind Hinkley Point C, declined to comment.

John Halsall, chief executive of South East Water, previously told The Mail, supplies in the region are now under 'severe pressure' and standpipes may appear on British streets for the first time in 50 years.

Mr Halsall also ended exemptions to a months-long hosepipe ban in Sussex – with the rules expected to be in place until next year.

However, he admitted his company's efforts to persuade customers to use less water had not been as successful as hoped – with water demand falling by about 5 per cent rather than around 10 per cent as expected.

Mr Halsall also recognised that outages in Tunbridge Wells and other towns and villages had undermined customer confidence.

Should the government impose tough water bans or let households and businesses decide how to cope?

Environment Agency chief executive Philip Duffy previously said: 'Record-breaking summer heat and severe drought have put our water resources under immense pressure.

'Recovery depends on both rainfall and how much water we use.

'We cannot change the weather, but water companies can act now. We expect them to follow their drought plans and take the steps needed to secure public water supplies.

'We are also asking everyone to use less water. That will leave more in our rivers and the environment, helping farmers and protecting wildlife and nature.'

The Environment Agency has granted South East Water’s drought permit application to reduce the amount of water Ardingly reservoir releases each day to the River Ouse by three million litres.

Separately, it has granted a drought permit application by South West Water to reduce the amount of water sent to the River Exe each day by nine million litres.

Last week a drought declaration was made for Yorkshire, with all of England now in drought except for the North West and North East which remain in prolonged dry weather status.

No risk of ammonia contamination, officials say, after fire at Oregon seafood plant

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

There was no further threat of ammonia contamination, officials said Sunday afternoon, after they earlier urged people to evacuate for fear of potential health risks following a seafood processing plant fire in coastal Oregon.

Authorities responded to the blaze at Pacific Seafood, a major seafood processing and distribution plant, in the city of Newport’s Bayfront neighborhood just before 9 a.m., city spokesperson John Fuller said at an afternoon news conference.

As of 4 p.m. local time, officials said there was no threat of ammonia contamination. The state fire marshal said that an ammonia tank had been found and that it didn’t seem to be punctured or leaking.

Newport Fire Chief Rob Murphy said at the news conference that while trace amounts of ammonia were initially detected, there was no ongoing risk of ammonia exposure.

“Between all the firefighters and our regional hazmat team, they did an excellent job of protecting those tanks and keeping them, you know, from something worse happening,” Murphy said. “I can tell you that the tanks are intact; they’re not leaking. The product inside them is stable and will remain so.”

As of Sunday afternoon, he said, the main environmental concern was smoke produced by the fire, which was still burning. There was still a small evacuation zone spanning blocks near the fire, he said.

The blaze had earlier triggered “level 3” evacuations, meaning “extreme danger in the area,” for those within a 1-mile radius of the plant. The city said the evacuation was “precautionary at the risk of a larger leak or spill.”

Ammonia is a toxic clear gas or liquid, and exposure to high levels can hurt the skin, eyes, throat and lungs, while exposure to very high levels can cause lung damage or death, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

The city earlier recommended that people stay inside and wear masks if they are outdoors. A shelter was set up at Atonement Lutheran Church for the displaced.

The city there were no impacts to marine life as of Sunday evening.

Photos and video from the scene show heavy smoke rising from a blue-painted building and fire trucks dousing it with water.

Fuller said that no injuries had been reported thus far and that there were 30 to 40 people in or near the facility at the time of the incident. He said that during peak tourist season in the summer, there can typically be as many as 200 people in the area.

One firefighter was treated for exhaustion, the city said.

Newport Mayor Jan Kaplan described Pacific Seafood as a critical part of the city’s economy and a “significant employer.”

Fuller, the city spokesperson, said that the fire erupted in a freezer facility where seafood is stored and that it did not spread into the main plant.

Ashton Meier, vice president of Pacific Seafood’s resource sales and processing, said in a statement that the company was “devastated” by the fire and expects “it will be a major loss.”

“As a family-owned business, we deeply value our people and our community,” Meier said. “We are eternally grateful for the help of the emergency responders today and thank them for all of their efforts. We are incredibly thankful and glad to report that all of our team members on site are safe and accounted for and that at this time we are not aware of any injuries to others in the area.”

Meier said the company will work with public safety officials to determine the cause of the fire and any hazards.
