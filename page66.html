¿Es posible que los planes de pensiones sean obligatorios?

📅 Oct 25, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Gestoras, aseguradoras, patronal, sindicatos y gobiernos buscan un pacto para perfilar un sistema efectivo de previsión que complemente la pensión pública.

El debate sobre las pensiones privadas vuelve a estar encima de la mesa tras la convocatoria de elecciones generales para el próximo 29 de noviembre.

La controversia tiene varias aristas e intereses contrapuestos que llevan años sin lograr un punto de encuentro efectivo, independiente del color del Gobierno en cada momento.

La posibilidad de acuerdo entre las partes solo se puede atisbar a través de un consenso político y social, algo que está a años luz de darse en España y que tampoco es muy previsible tras las elecciones, gane quien gane.

Las gestoras de pensiones y las aseguradoras son la parte más activa en un escenario en el que sindicatos y empresas tienen un papel determinante igual que el Estado y el Ejecutivo de turno.

La adhesión obligatoria (autoenrollment) de todos los trabajadores a un plan de pensiones de empleo, el modelo implantando en Reino Unido e Irlanda, se ha hecho hueco en las reivindicaciones más recientes de gestoras y aseguradoras.

Pero no es la única alternativa propuesta por la parte privada para configurar un pilar complementario al sistema público de pensiones.

Las entidades llevan décadas poniendo el foco en los esquemas implantados en otros países. La mochila austriaca, el modelo sueco, con su sobre naranja; o la alternativa chilena son algunos referentes señalados, sin olvidarse de las EPSV (entidades de previsión voluntaria) del País Vasco, que cuentan con mejor fiscalidad y mayores aportaciones. Su penetración entre los trabajadores es mayor que la de los planes de pensiones en el resto del Estado entre otras cosas por su mayor tradición en el mundo de las cooperativas y su cultura de negociación colectiva.

Las gestoras a través de Inverco y las compañías de seguros a través de Unespa son inasequibles al desaliento en su empeño por ampliar la actividad de su negocio, ante lo que consideran la imposibilidad de mantener en el futuro las actuales pensiones públicas.

Desde la Administración, de diferentes signo político, se han reducido año tras año las vías de desarrollo de esta actividad, con cancelación de aportaciones a los empleados públicos y límites al ahorro y a sus beneficios fiscales a los particulares.

En paralelo, se han ampliado los incentivos a las pensiones de empleo por considerar que son las que pueden llegar a más población, pero la respuesta de este colectivo es muy moderada.

Son los sindicatos, con CCOO como el más activo, y CEOE, quienes tienen la llave para que el eterno debate de la previsión social complementaria salga del día de la marmota en el que lleva instalado desde hace décadas. "Así llevamos año, tras año, tras año" dijo Olimpia del Águila, responsable de seguridad social e igualdad del departamento de empleo, protección social e igualdad de CEOE, en el II Observatorio de Pensiones Grupo Mutua, celebrado el jueves pasado.

La posición de la Unión Europea a favor de estos mecanismos de previsión social complementaria puede ayudar a salir de este bucle, afirmó Ángel Martínez Aldama, presidente de Inverco, en este encuentro.

Carlos Bravo, secretario de políticas públicas y seguridad social de CCOO, dejó la puerta abierta al modelo británico de adhesión automática, que calificó de "interesante" y del que dijo que "podría dar juego" en su intervención en este encuentro.

Este modelo fija una aportación total del 8% del salario a los planes de pensiones de empleo que se distribuye entre un 4% por parte del empleado, un 3% de la empresa y un 1% del Estado.

El representante de CCOO apostilló que para calibrar la situación hay que tener en cuenta que Reino Unido, Irlanda y Australia, que también cuenta con pensiones privadas obligatorias para los trabajadores, tienen una tasa de sustitución (porcentaje que supone la prestación de jubilación sobre el último salario) de entre el 29% y el 34% muy inferior a la española, que supera el 70%.

La implantación de la adhesión obligatoria debe contar con el visto bueno de los empresarios y para CEOE, el autoenrollment ya está incorporado en España a través de la negociación colectiva. "Es una forma de superar la dificultad de adscribir obligatoriamente a los empleados a un plan de pensiones", ya que la Constitución española dice que las pensiones complementarias serán libres, "pero somos libres de pactar a través de la negociación colectiva la adscripción de nuestras empresas y trabajadores de un sector" (ver información adjunta).

No obstante, la representante de los empresarios, se mostró reticente a aumentar el coste que el autoenrollment supone para las empresas, porque "no se puede hacer más caro el mantenimiento de los puestos de trabajo".

Como si de un juego de espejos se tratara, los sindicatos tampoco están por la labor de que la aportación de los trabajadores se detraiga de su nómina, en su opinión debe ser adicional: un nuevo salario diferido.

Cada parte defiende sus intereses y en esta línea Ángel Martínez Aldama, presidente de Inverco propuso en que se ordene la obligatoriedad de las pensiones privadas en aras de preservar de la salud financiera de la población igual que en su día se prohibió el consumo de tabaco en determinados espacios para proteger la salud de los ciudadanos o, en la misma línea, se implantó la retirada de los puntos del carnet de conducir para tratar de reducir los accidentes de tráfico.

Reivindicaciones aparte, lo que parece claro es que la última reforma de las pensiones privadas, impulsada en 2022 por el actual gobernador del Banco de España y ex ministro de Inclusión y Seguridad Social y Migraciones, no ha funcionado.

Se lanzó sin acuerdo con las partes implicadas y aunque el objetivo de extender la previsión social complementaria a todos los trabajadores fuera compartido, no lo era el camino para lograrlo.

"Después de 16 años de parálisis absoluta del sector, en 2022 se abrió una ventana de oportunidad", señala Bravo que apunta, no obstante, a deficiencias en el planteamiento inicial.

En su opinión, en el fracaso de esta iniciativa "hay una responsabilidad compartida del legislador, de los interlocutores sociales y del sector".

El Gobierno lanzó el proyecto "sin consenso interno" entre el ministerio de Escrivá y el de Hacienda y "por lo tanto con la falta de acompañamiento con medidas fiscales" sostiene.

Además, añadió Bravo, la reforma nace con el objetivo de impulsar la previsión social entre los funcionarios y los autónomos. En el primer caso, se da la "incongruencia de que la Administración no ha habilitado las aportaciones al sector público", suspendidas desde la crisis financiera.

En paralelo, dijo, los autónomos no están aprovechando los incentivos fiscales que da la reforma a sus aportaciones a pensiones. "Yo creo que aquí hay un interés por parte de los comercializadores de estos productos que prefieren retener en el tercer pilar a este colectivo, con retornos más altos que en los planes de pensiones simplificados".

En el reparto de responsabilidades, Comisiones Obreras reconoce que sindicatos y patronal no están analizando la implantación de pensiones complementarias en la negociación colectiva, a excepción del sector de la construcción, "un hito con el que estamos encantados".

La Constitución española establece en su artículo 41 el carácter libre de las prestaciones complementarias a la pensión pública. Esto choca con la adscripción automática para todos los trabajadores, incluida en el autoenrollment. The game opened up, and Yan Diomande came close to doubling Madrid’s lead before half-time, drilling into the side netting.

Martin redeemed himself early in the second half as he held off Jude Bellingham, who could neither beat the defender nor find a free Mbappe.

Raphinha, who had a quiet first half, pulled Barcelona level just after the hour mark.

Pedri fed the Brazilian, La Liga’s top goalscorer with 13, who fired through Bellingham’s legs, and Lunin could not keep the shot out.

The Ukrainian goalkeeper did make a superb save to deny Martin a corner as Barcelona flowed forward with the wind finally in their sails.

Yamal bent an effort wide with Lunin at full stretch, and Barca substitute Frenkie de Jong had a shot desperately blocked by Dean Huijsen.

There was a sting in the tail for the Catalans, though, as substitute Gabriel Jesus lost possession and Madrid raced through on goal.

Eduardo Camavinga beat Cubarsi and unselfishly cut the ball back for Mbappe to tuck home his second of the night into an empty net, with Joan Garcia drawn out of position.

Kimi Antonelli demonstrated his title-winning prowess with a thumping victory at the United States Grand Prix but his coronation as champion was put on ice.

For the title to be wrapped up, the Italian needed major slip ups from his rivals. Lewis Hamilton extended the championship race by finishing fourth and George Russell just about did his bit despite only managing a disappointing eighth.

Antonelli will head to Mexico City next week with a whopping 123-point lead over Russell, with Hamilton a point further back. A finish of ninth or higher will seal the title, no matter where his rivals finish.

He leapt from his Mercedes and launched himself into the arms of his mechanics for the ninth time in this dominant season.

Leclerc claimed second for successive weeks after an impressive race for Ferrari, making a late move on Max Verstappen who completed the podium.

Lando Norris made a one-stop strategy work to claim fifth for McLaren.

Antonelli believed it would take something “catastrophic” from his rivals for him to clinch the crown in Texas, but knew winning the race was required for his end of the bargain.

He then needed Russell, whose faint title challenge has evaporated over recent races, to finish ninth or lower and Hamilton to be fifth or lower.

Part one of Antonelli’s job was done on Saturday as he soared to a magnificent pole as the sun began to drop on the Circuit of the Americas. With Hamilton only fifth and Russell sixth, his chances of getting over the line increased.

The four-time world champion was soon up to speed and rocketed up the inside of Leclerc to reclaim second but Antonelli had already checked out – 2.4 seconds ahead at the end of the opening lap.

Russell’s role in the celebration-delaying picture was hampered as he was investigated for leaving the track and staying ahead of Oscar Piastri – with whom he banged wheels as he rejoined.

A five-second penalty was dished out to the Mercedes man. In a race fraught with jeopardy, Russell and Hamilton were now both on the cusp of handing Antonelli his crowning moment.

Verstappen was the first to pull the trigger on a pit stop on lap 14, swiftly followed by Hamilton and Leclerc but Antonelli – who started on the more durable medium tyres – and Mercedes chose not to respond.

Russell pitted and served his penalty on lap 24, rejoining a net eighth before Antonelli pitted a lap later and was now a net third – on much fresher tyres than those ahead.

New race leader Verstappen was showing strong pace, while Hamilton was promoted to fourth when Hadjar boxed.

The Dutchman pitted for a second time with 25 laps remaining, as Antonelli charged through on Leclerc to lead once more.

Leclerc and Hamilton stopped again in the final 16 laps, rejoining fourth and sixth – with the British driver needing to make up two places to ensure he kept Antonelli from the trophy.

He dispatched Hadjar swiftly but Norris had done a magnificent job on a one-stop strategy. The reigning champion – about to yield his crown – was reeled in on lap 47 by Verstappen and Leclerc, and Hamilton rose to fourth the following lap.

All the action was taking place behind Antonelli, who marched on serenely out front.

Antonelli has been the standout performer of the 2026 season and will be crowned a worthy champion over the coming weeks, fittingly shining brightest once again in the Lone Star State.

KAMPALA, Oct 25 - Ugandan citizens returning to the country with a history of travel to Democratic Republic of Congo will be quarantined for 21 days, Health Minister Chris Baryomunsi said on Sunday as Uganda tightened Ebola precautions.

Uganda says it is Ebola-free after declaring an end to its latest outbreak in July, but there has been concern among health experts after two people who had recently passed through the country later tested positive in Kenya and Nigeria.

Uganda says it is investigating the Nigerian case, which involves the wife of a Swiss diplomat. The woman, 42, returned from Uganda through Addis Ababa to Abuja on Oct. 3, according to Nigeria's Centre for Disease Control, which said she is in isolation and has improved remarkably.

The agency said 53 contacts had been traced.

Ugandan officials are reviewing genomic sequencing, travel information, interactions and movements in coordination with Nigerian authorities and international partners.

Swiss authorities had said the woman is believed to have contracted the virus in Uganda. Baryomunsi pushed back, saying Switzerland was "speaking prematurely". He said the woman's relatives from West Nile visited her in Kansanga, a suburb of Kampala, and that those relatives had visited Congo.

"What evidence do they have that she never travelled there?" he said. "We should speak science, not politics."

Kenya's first Ebola patient had travelled from Congo to Uganda and taken a flight from Entebbe airport to Nairobi on Oct. 3. The patient died two days later.

Both the Kenyan and the Nigerian cases travelled through the same Ugandan airport on the same day but there was no physical interaction between the two, Diana Atwine, the health ministry's permanent secretary, told the press conference, citing a review of the airport's CCTV system.

Baryomunsi said all 60 contacts linked to a Kenyan Ebola case had completed their 21-day monitoring period and been discharged.

"The ministry has intensified traveller screening at Entebbe International Airport and all land border crossings. All travellers intending to enter Uganda by air, land or water must complete the passenger locator form," he said.

Genomic analysis of Nigeria's only confirmed Ebola case showed the virus was closely related to the strain detected in Kenya's index case, though Nigerian authorities said there was no evidence that the Kenya case travelled to Nigeria or had a direct connection.

Uganda borders Congo, where the outbreak began, and has previously battled Ebola outbreaks.

The Israeli military pressed ahead on Sunday (October 25, 2026) with a massive military operation in the occupied West Bank that has displaced hundreds of Palestinians and placed major restrictions on movement in response to a gun attack last week that killed two Israeli soldiers and wounded three others.

The operation, focused on the city of Hebron, the site of Friday’s attack, came as Israel enters the final days before a tense election, which is largely seen as a referendum on current Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s leadership and security policies during the past three years of war.

In new measures, the Army said that most Palestinian workers with permits will not be allowed to enter Israeli West Bank settlements on Sunday and Monday, and crossings into Israel will remain closed until after Israel’s elections on Tuesday.

The military said it operated in more than 400 different locations over the weekend and arrested 40 Palestinians it claimed were affiliated with the Hamas or Islamic Jihad militant groups. No militant group has taken responsibility for the attack.

The Hebron municipality said the operation displaced more than 2,600 people.

The military said that troops were conducting searches in the area where the gunmen operated, and warned residents to evacuate Deir Bahha, the suspected gunmen’s neighborhood. After Friday’s attack, Israel’s military said it found weapons in the gunmen’s car and that they were likely planning to attack Israeli civilians before being stopped at the checkpoint where the shootout erupted.

All entrances and exits to the city remained sealed, with the operation focused in Deir Bahha and another part of the city called Al-Jalda, according to Hebron Mayor Yousef Zuhair Al-Jabari.

“People cannot reach or enter the city, and they cannot leave it. The blockade has affected every aspect of life,” Mr. Al-Jabari said. He added that the blockade is forcing businesses to close and has also shuttered a mosque.

“The army told us that this area would be closed for 12 days. Now they’re saying 12 days, but maybe they’ll leave the area after two or three days,” said Shadi al-Qawasmi, a Hebron resident who was displaced with his family. “This may be because of the elections or some political matter. No one knows except God Almighty.”

The Army declined to say how long the operation might last.
