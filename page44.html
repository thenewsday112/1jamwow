Объяснено устаревание умных телевизоров

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Фото: janvasekcz / Shutterstock / Fotodom

Устаревание исправных умных телевизоров объяснили ограниченным сроком программной поддержки и изменением требований приложений. Об этом сообщает издание How-To Geek.

Автор материала Кори Гюнтер рассказал, что телевизор может сохранять качественное изображение и исправно работать как экран, но постепенно терять доступ к онлайн-сервисам. Причина не обязательно в поломке: производитель перестает обновлять программное обеспечение, а разработчики приложений со временем прекращают поддержку старых устройств.

Журналист разобрал ситуацию на примере Google TV. Телевизоры с этой платформой выпускают разные компании, поэтому одинаковый интерфейс не означает одинаковый срок обновлений. Условия поддержки необходимо проверять для конкретной модели и региона, а не ориентироваться только на название операционной системы.

Для проверки оставшегося срока поддержки специалисты посоветовали найти полное обозначение модели на корпусе или в настройках телевизора, а затем открыть ее страницу на сайте производителя. Следует искать сведения об обновлениях безопасности и дате окончания поддержки. Отсутствие доступной новой прошивки само по себе еще не доказывает, что устройство окончательно заброшено.

Ранее журналисты издания Computer Bild предупредили пользователей о риске опрокидывания больших телевизоров. Они отметили, что большой экран может иметь сравнительно узкую подставку, а центр тяжести устройства располагается высоко над поверхностью мебели.

Lula da Silva has been declared the winner of the Brazilian election, in a result that will send shockwaves throughout South America.

The left-wing candidate defeated Senator Flavio Bolsonaro, the eldest son of former President Jair Bolsonaro. The senator had finished first in the election's first round and hoped to revive his father's far-right movement.

At times, Lula's reelection looked like a long shot, as voters soured on him and his Workers' Party after years of corruption scandals and rising prices, while sweeping societal changes left the former metalworker and union leader looking out of touch with an increasingly app-based economy and its growing ranks of informal workers.

Lula's victory marks another comeback in a career full of them. The 80-year-old was jailed from 2018 to 2019 on corruption charges that were later overturned, paving the way for him to beat Jair Bolsonaro in the 2022 election.

"He is a statesman, a giant," leftist federal lawmaker Erika Hilton, who was with Lula in Sao Paulo during the vote count, told Reuters

Pollsters said better-than-expected turnout had powered Lula to victory. Leonardo Barreto, the co-founder of consultancy Think Policy, said voters had chosen Lula's familiar embrace over Bolsonaro's novelty.

"People today are choosing between change, with some apprehension, and a known path, which guarantees certain rights," he said.

During the campaign, Lula pointed to a track record of falling poverty and inequality, while unemployment, illiteracy and deforestation hit record lows during his third term.

He warned Bolsonaro would cut welfare spending, backslide on social advances and hand over Brazil's sovereignty to US President Donald Trump, whose State Department led a campaign to get the Bolsonaros back to power.

Lula's win deprives Trump of a major addition to what has been a growing bloc of friendly right-wing Latin American governments that have embraced Washington's agenda on security and migration.

Celso Amorim, a former Lula foreign minister, told Reuters the leftist "would be very important for establishing a point of balance in Latin America. Lula is that balance."

Lula gewinnt Wahl - Bolsonaro erkennt Niederlage nicht an

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Der linke Staatschef Lula hat die Präsidentschaftswahl in Brasilien gewonnen. Er setzte sich gegen den rechten Kandidaten Bolsonaro durch - doch der will das Ergebnis nicht anerkennen.

Der brasilianische Präsident Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva ist nach Angaben der Wahlbehörde für eine vierte Amtszeit gewählt worden. Er gewann demnach in Stichwahl gegen Flávio Bolsonaro, den Sohn des unter Hausarrest stehenden rechtsextremen Ex-Präsidenten Jair Bolsonaro.

Lula kam demnach auf 51,8 Prozent und Bolsonaro auf 48,2. Damit holte der Amtsinhaber fast 4,4 Millionen mehr Stimmen als Bolsonaro, wie das Oberste Wahlgericht mitteilte. Meinungsforschern zufolge verhalf eine unerwartet hohe Wahlbeteiligung Lula zum Sieg.

Bolsonaro weigerte sich, seine Niederlage gegen Lula einzugestehen. "Es ist unmöglich, das Ergebnis anzuerkennen", sagte Bolsonaro in Brasilia. "Ich werde die Annullierung dieser Wahlen beantragen", fügte Bolsonaro hinzu.

Bolsonaro begründete seine Forderung mit der kurzfristigen Entscheidung des Obersten Gerichtshofs, die Wahllokale zwei Stunden länger geöffnet zu lassen. Die Entscheidung sei beispiellos gewesen, erklärte er.

Allerdings hatten beide Kandidaten beantragt, Wahllokale länger geöffnet zu halten, Bolsonaro aber nur in bestimmten Bundesstaaten. Hintergrund waren Probleme im öffentlichen Nahverkehr sowie schlechtes Wetter in Teilen des Landes.

Lula, der diese Woche 81 Jahre alt wird, bezeichnete seinen Wahlsieg auch als Abstimmung über die Demokratie. Die Brasilianer hätten dafür gestimmt, "die Demokratie zu sichern", sagte Lula. Auf den Straßen feierten Tausende Anhänger seinen Sieg.

Die Wahl in Lateinamerikas größter Volkswirtschaft galt als Richtungsentscheidung. Lula und Bolsonaro vertreten in zahlreichen Politikfeldern gegensätzliche Positionen, etwa bei den Beziehungen zu den USA, beim Klimaschutz und in der Sozialpolitik.

Bolsonaro war von seinem Vater ins Rennen geschickt worden. Jair Bolsonaro ist wegen eines Umsturzversuchs zu mehr als 27 Jahren Haft verurteilt worden. Aus gesundheitlichen Gründen steht der frühere Präsident derzeit unter Hausarrest.

Covert recording of prison meeting between Beau Lamarre-Condon and friend played to double murder trial

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Politics  |  Read Source →

Former police officer accused of shooting dead Jesse Baird and Luke Davies apologised to friend, acknowledging ‘you’re involved because of me’

A covert recording captured Beau Lamarre-Condon telling a friend during a meeting in prison in December last year, “I fully take accountability that you’re involved because of me now,” a Sydney court has heard.

Lamarre-Condon, 31, is accused of fatally shooting couple Jesse Baird and Luke Davies with his police service weapon in Baird’s Paddington home on 19 February 2024.

He has pleaded not guilty to two counts of murder and one charge of aggravated break and enter.

Lamarre-Condon’s friend, whose identity is suppressed, told the New South Wales supreme court on Friday that Lamarre-Condon allegedly confessed to killing the two men, and told his friend it was an “out-of-body experience”, before then attempting to play it off as a joke.

On Monday, the friend was back in the witness stand, while a recording was played to the court of a conversation the friend recorded on behalf of police when he visited Lamarre-Condon in prison. At the beginning of the conversation, Lamarre-Condon asked the friend to turn around and show he was not wearing a wire.

“You’re so paranoid,” the friend said to Lamarre-Condon in the recording played to the jury. “Calm down.”

The friend said to Lamarre-Condon during the prison visit: “You put me in a scenario that’s very difficult for me.”

Lamarre-Condon apologised, stating, “I fully take accountability that you’re involved because of me now,” the court heard on Monday.

The friend, in the recording, said he was upset because Lamarre-Condon had said he was dumb. In response, Lamarre-Condon said “You’re not dumb OK,” before then telling his friend: “You’re not self aware in the way that I am.”

Lamarre-Condon accused his friend during the conversation of lying “about so much shit” in his statement to police. He said his friend was wrong to recollect that Lamarre-Condon had said Davies was killed to eliminate a witness and in fact he’d acted in self-defence.

“Don’t you dare switch up,” the friend responded during the prison visit.

Lamarre-Condon in the recording told his friend his entry into Baird’s home was not break and enter because Baird had got a key cut for him. He admitted that entering the Paddington terrace was trespass but claimed it was normal to return to a former partner’s home to retrieve belongings. He said he did not knock on the door to get the belongings back because he didn’t want to be slapped with an apprehended violence order, but he did call out hello when he walked inside.

Lamarre-Condon, in the recording played to the jury, told his friend: “If I had planned to do this mate, I’d be cool, calm, collected.”

He said he would have had “cleaning ready to go” and he would not have used a police firearm.

“I can get [another gun] for $10,000, I could have done all these things,” he said in the recording played to court.

Lamarre-Condon told the friend he took him to the Royal national park before the murders in early 2024 and shot his gun to test how loud the gunshots were because he was planning to return there to take his own life.

He told the friend during the recording that he had been suffering from suicidality from trauma at work.

During the phone call, the friend can be heard threatening to leave multiple times.

“What you’re telling me mightn’t be the truth,” the friend said.

During cross-examination, Lamarre-Condon’s lawyer, John Stratton SC, suggested it was the friend’s idea to sink Bair and Davies’ bodies in a dam.

“I’m 100% sure it wasn’t me,” the friend told the court on Monday.

He said he warned Lamarre-Condon the bodies would float. “I gave him a scientific fact that he [could] search up on Google in his own time,” the witness said.

The friend admitted he told Lamarre-Condon – after the pair had discussed if the bodies should be taken to a national park – that bushland was nearby in the Royal national park.

The friend denied Stratton’s suggestion that he had given false evidence and was influenced by media reporting.

The trial before Justice Natalie Adams continues.

Top officials from Mecca Pact countries to meet Iranian officials in Tehran: Sources

Ministers from the Mecca Pact countries met up in Riyadh earlier in October, following an escalation of attack on Saudi Arabia by the Iran-aligned Houthis in Yemen.

Top officials from the Mecca Pact countries Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan are set to meet Iranian officials in Tehran on Oct 26 to discuss regional coordination, two people with knowledge of the matter said.

Saudi Arabia will be represented by Defence Minister Prince Khalid bin Salman, and Pakistan by army chief Field Marshal Asim Munir, the people said on condition of anonymity.

The Turkish head of intelligence will also join, one of the people said.

The Saudi government media office, Pakistan’s military media wing, and Turkey’s foreign ministry did not immediately respond to Reuters’ requests for comment.

The planned talks would be the first high-level contact between Iran and all members of the three-nation bloc since Saudi Arabia has come under escalating attacks from Yemen’s Iran-aligned Houthis.

Munir visited Tehran last week, where he met Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian and other senior officials. During the visit, Iranian officials told him there would be no talks with Washington until Tehran’s demands were met, according to Iranian media.

Pakistan has been a key intermediary between the United States and Iran. Earlier mediation efforts helped produce an interim peace agreement in June, but the deal quickly unravelled as fighting between Iran and the United States resumed weeks later.

Saudi Arabia intercepted another wave of missiles and drones on Oct 25 as fighting with the militant group continued unabated.

Ministers from the Mecca Pact countries held an emergency meeting in Riyadh earlier in October and agreed to ensure the “rapid deployment” of military forces and capabilities in Saudi Arabia.

The conflict has become an increasingly destabilising element in the US-Israeli offensive against Iran, turning US ally Saudi Arabia into a pressure point ⁠in the wider war.

Turkey, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia signed the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement in August, under which an armed attack on one member is considered an attack on all.

The agreement’s first article says the parties shall use “all necessary means” to eliminate threats to one another’s territorial integrity, sovereignty or internationally recognised borders, according to a text seen by Reuters.

Efforts by Turkey’s Parliament to ratify the agreement began last week and are still underway, while Pakistan approved the pact last week. REUTERS

Serbia has entered a new political era after Aleksandar Vucic conceded defeat to a historic student-led protest movement in an election on Sunday, bringing an end to his long grip on power.

The streets of Belgrade erupted in celebration following Vucic's announcement, as drivers sounded their car horns and students chanted "victory" and "he's done".

"It is clear that we have lost these elections, the Student List has won, they will have between 135 and 140 seats and we will have 105, no one else has entered parliament," Vucic said, basing his projection off preliminary results.

A representative sample from pollsters CESID-IPSPS showed the student candidate list winning 138 deputies, with Vucic's SNS party securing 104 seats in the 250-seat parliament. The remaining seats will be shared among ethnic national minority parties.

"Unbelievable happiness and joy and we have finally got rid of this mafia. I am so happy I cannot speak," said a woman named Tanja who was celebrating on the streets of Belgrade.

Simba faces possible fines, licence cancellation over allegations of intentional spectrum misuse

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Simba's unauthorised spectrum use was allegedly intentional and approved by senior management. The telco is also accused of deliberately failing to fully comply with an order to produce documents.

A Simba shop at Canberra Plaza in Singapore on Aug 18, 2025. (Photo: CNA/Ooi Boon Keong)

SINGAPORE: Telecommunications operator Simba could face fines, suspension or cancellation of its licence over alleged unauthorised spectrum use and other regulatory breaches, according to new details of an ongoing investigation disclosed by its Australian parent company Tuas on Monday (Oct 26).

In a filing to the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX), Tuas said the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) had issued Simba a notice to show cause, setting out six allegations involving unauthorised spectrum use, breaches of telecommunications regulations and concerns about the company's governance and compliance practices.

These include allegations that Simba's unauthorised use of radio frequency bands was intentional and approved by its senior management, and that the telco deliberately failed to fully comply with an order to produce documents.

The filing also outlined potential regulatory actions being considered by IMDA, including financial penalties and the suspension or cancellation of Simba's spectrum rights or telecommunications licence.

IMDA previously disclosed in May that it was investigating Simba over possible unauthorised spectrum use, prompting it to suspend its review of the telco's proposed S$1.43 billion (US$1.11 billion) acquisition of rival operator M1.

According to Monday's filing, IMDA alleged that Simba deployed dynamic spectrum sharing technology at about 133 sites for intermittent periods in 2022 and 2023.

This allowed the telco to use the 2100 megahertz (MHz) frequency band for both 4G and 5G services, even though it was authorised to use the band only for 5G.

Dynamic spectrum sharing is a technology that allows different generations of mobile networks, such as 4G and 5G, to share the same frequency band.

IMDA also alleged that Simba used spectrum in the 2340MHz to 2360MHz band at about 92 sites for intermittent periods between 2023 and 2026, despite not having authorisation to do so.

The regulator alleged that both instances of unauthorised spectrum use were intentional and approved by Simba's senior management.

Separately, IMDA alleged that Simba deployed 5G indoor radio access network equipment at two locations in 2023 before receiving authorisation to do so. Approval was only granted on Nov 14 that year.

The regulator also alleged that Simba did not fully comply with an order to produce documents, and that its failure to do so was deliberate and intentional.

IMDA further raised concerns about deficiencies in Simba's governance and compliance culture, which it considers should be addressed through additional regulatory obligations.

IMDA could impose financial penalties of up to 10 per cent of the annual turnover of the relevant part of Simba's business or S$1 million, whichever is higher.

Other possible actions include suspending or cancelling Simba's spectrum rights or telecommunications licence, shortening its licence period, or imposing additional regulatory obligations to address governance deficiencies.

Simba has until Nov 16 to respond to the show cause notice.

Tuas noted that IMDA has not reached any conclusions on whether regulatory action should be taken against Simba or what form such action might take.

"Simba intends to respond to the Show Cause Notice," the company said.

"As the matter remains under consideration by the regulator and no findings have been made or confirmed, Tuas does not intend to provide further comment on the matters raised in this announcement."

IMDA first disclosed its investigation into Simba in May, saying it had uncovered possible unauthorised spectrum use by the telco.

The regulator suspended its review of Simba's proposed S$1.43 billion acquisition of M1, saying the findings of its investigation could be material to its assessment of the deal.

The proposed acquisition, which would have consolidated two of Singapore's four mobile network operators, was subsequently terminated after the deadline for its completion passed.

Monday's filing sets out more specific allegations, including that certain instances of unauthorised spectrum use were intentional and approved by senior management.

Radio frequency spectrum refers to the electromagnetic waves used to carry wireless communications, including mobile phone calls and data.

IMDA regulates the allocation and assignment of spectrum to telecommunications operators, which are authorised to use specific frequency bands for their services.

These rules are intended to prevent interference between wireless services and ensure that operators use spectrum in accordance with their assigned rights and licence conditions.

Während Präsident Emmanuel Macron und seine Mitte-Rechts-Regierung von vielen Seiten der Bevölkerung Wut auf sich ziehen, legt Le Pen in der neuesten Ifop-Umfrage seit den Ausschreitungen mit Blick auf die Wahl in rund einem halben Jahr weiter zu.

Demnach erreicht sie je nach Kandidatenkonstellation 34 bis 38 Prozent der Stimmen im ersten Wahlgang – ein bis zwei Punkte mehr als vorher. In der zweiten Wahlrunde steht Le Pen in sämtlichen Szenarien der Untersuchung als Gewinnerin da.

Ob die Unruhen den Zuwachs verursacht haben, lässt sich aus den Daten nicht ableiten. Es ist aber eine gute Ausgangsposition für die 58-Jährige, die nach RN-Angaben vor rund 8500 Anhängerinnen und Anhängern in Orléans am Rednerpult stand.

In ihrer Rede sprach sich Le Pen unter dem Motto der «Wiedergeburt» («Renaissance») für Frankreich unter anderem für harte Migrationspolitik, die Bekämpfung krimineller Organisationen und nationale Souveränität aus. Dabei gab sie sich schon jetzt stellenweise präsidial: Sie wolle Soziales nicht gegen Wirtschaft, Rechte nicht gegen Linke ausspielen.

«Ich bin weder der rechte Flügel noch der linke Flügel. Ich bin der Vogel», so die Politikerin. Für die Ausschreitungen vom 17. Oktober hatte sie zuvor die Regierung und die Einwanderungspolitik verantwortlich gemacht und ein härteres Durchgreifen der Behörden gefordert.

Die Proteste in Frankreich setzen den innenpolitisch ohnehin geschwächten Macron und die Regierung unter Druck, auch wenn die Behörden am zweiten Demonstrationswochenende grösstenteils die Kontrolle behielten. Für kommenden Samstag sind wieder Kundgebungen angekündigt.

Ein anderes Wahlkampfthema, nicht nur für Le Pen, werden Frankreichs Finanzen. Der Staat ist so hoch verschuldet wie noch nie. Um ihre finanzpolitische Glaubwürdigkeit zu untermauern, stellte Le Pen einen alternativen Haushaltsplan mit milliardenschweren Einsparungen vor. Frankreichs Nettobeitrag zur EU will sie etwa auf maximal fünf Milliarden Euro kürzen. Regierung, Experten und Wirtschaftsmedien kritisierten den Plan als unrealistisch und ungenau.

Der RN-Kandidatin könnte in die Karten spielen, dass sich bei den anderen politischen Gruppierungen noch ein regelrechtes Wimmelbild an Kandidatinnen und Kandidaten zeigt. Insbesondere das Mitte-Lager hat sich noch nicht klar sortiert.

Die Ifop-Umfrage sieht andere Bewerber wie den Anführer von Frankreichs Linkspartei Jean-Luc Mélenchon, Sozialdemokrat Raphaël Glucksmann, Mitte-Politiker Édouard Philippe, Gabriel Attal von der liberalen Renaissance oder Grünen-Kandidatin Marine Tondelier weit hinter Le Pen. Amtsinhaber Macron kann nicht mehr antreten.

Am 18. April 2027 entscheiden die Menschen in Frankreich, wer neuer Präsident oder neue Präsidentin wird. Erhält niemand die absolute Mehrheit der Stimmen, fällt die Entscheidung in einer Stichwahl am 2. Mai.
