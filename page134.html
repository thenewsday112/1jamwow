Ricardo Gómez se ha pasado casi toda la vida delante de la cámara. Y ahí, mientras crecía en frente de millones de personas, empezó a darse cuenta de que su curiosidad hacia lo que pasaba en un rodaje era más que un juego infantil. Comenzó a imaginar cómo rodaría él las escenas de Cuéntame, y luego cuando llegaba al set de rodaje veía si coincidía o cómo lo planteaban los directores con los que fue formándose casi por osmosis.

Ricardo Gómez debuta como director con 'Una familia', un viaje a sus recuerdos de la infancia y un homenaje a las madres

“Empecé a darme cuenta de que estaba planificando en mi cabeza escenas de la serie y luego casi nunca se hacían así. Un día pensé, joder, me fliparía poner a mí las cámaras. Supongo que de ahí nace todo, de estar durante muchos años entre cámaras viendo cómo otros dirigían y aportaban su punto de vista y pensar que más que ser solo una herramienta para contar una historia, me gustaría ser el motor de una de ellas”, dice al pensar en el momento en el que el gusanillo del director de cine empezó a colarse en sus entrañas.

Lo recuerda a pocos días de presentar su primera película como director, Una familia, un filme de tintes autobiográficos que es también una carta de amor a las madres (en especial a la suya) y que se presenta fuera de concurso en la Sección Oficial de Seminci. Una primera película que muestra a un director con ideas, que no se conforma con ir a lo seguro, y que en ese trenzado de historias consigue una hermosa película que deja con ganas de saber más de él como cineasta. Aquellas largas jornadas de rodaje durante años fueron su escuela de cine, y las toma así también para “callar a ese pequeño diablo del impostor” que le dice que no ha estudiado cine. “Me pasó lo mismo con la actuación. Yo no he ido a la RESAD ni he estudiado con Corazza, y me decía que cómo iba a ser actor sin haber estudiado”, explica.

Ese diablillo le sale de vez en cuando, y le ataca, pero Ricardo Gómez le da argumentos. “Le digo: bueno, no he ido a ninguna escuela, pero sí que he tenido formación por otros lados, más allá de la propia práctica. Con la dirección me pasa un poco igual, sé que el paso natural hubiese sido hacer un corto con más financiación y no me salió. Así que me puse a escribir. Me parece supercomplicado escribir un cortometraje y no ser un tramposo de guion. Admiro mucho a los cortometrajistas”, subraya.

Los nervios que vive ahora no son los mismos que siente antes de salir al escenario de un teatro o de rodar una escena. “Esta semana estoy hablando conmigo mismo y pienso, no es la semana de estreno, pero sí es la primera vez que se va a ver. Va a haber un teatro lleno. La estáis viendo la prensa… y me digo: ¿por qué estás tan nervioso? No lo entiendo. He dirigido una peli, pero, por otro lado, llevo estrenando cosas toda la vida. Pero es que esto es distinto. Es distinto porque como actor hay algo de decir 'bueno, pues mira, si esta peli no la hubiese hecho yo igual tendría otra cara y otros ojos'. Pero aquí hay algo muy fundamental, y es que sin mí esto no hubiese existido. Y esto está existiendo porque yo he querido, y esta gente está aquí sentada y entregando 95 minutos a esta historia. Entonces hay algo como casi de responsabilidad que me pone más nervioso”, confiesa.

La historia de Una familia juega con un elemento reconocible para todo el mundo. Un niño, con el pelo a tazón, acaba el rodaje de una escena de una serie que presenta a una familia con su abuela, su padre con bigote, y todos en un salón clásico de familia española. Un claro homenaje a Cuéntame y a su propia vida que es un gancho de autoficción para lo que viene, tres historias que se entrelazan. La de un padre ausente que en un permiso de la cárcel deja embarazada a una bailarina. La de ambos el día del bautizo cuando ambos ya han tenido a ese niño. Y la de ese propio niño convertido en un actor infantil de éxito con una vida en pleno cambio y una familia que no se parece en nada a la que muestra la serie que protagoniza.

Yo necesito mirar hacia adelante y cuando hay algo que tiene una sombra tan larga como esto, la única manera de intentar avanzar es mirando en otra dirección

Tres historias entrelazadas que, de alguna forma, ofrecen al espectador un dulce que para él “es parte del juego de la ficción”. “Es una película de ficción. Nunca me ha interesado hacer un documental. La película parte de ese elemento que es hiperreconocible y no se estanca. Ahí la película avanza hacia otro sitio. Hace mucho tiempo que no trato de controlar la opinión del espectador y para mí tiene más que ver con un juego de ficción y realidad. Y tiene mucho que ver con la fábula. De hecho, la película está un poco rodada como una fábula en el sentido de que no es hiperrealista, tiene movimientos muy estéticos de cámara”, explica.

Pese a ello sí que la película “parte de una base muy autobiográfica” que Ricardo Gómez no esconde. De hecho, la escritura de guion le llevó también a una investigación en la historia de su propia familia y, en concreto, de su madre. “Nunca había tenido con mi madre un momento de charla. Fueron unas entrevistas en donde le dije: quiero hablar de ti, de tu familia, de muchas cosas que creo que sé, porque sí, las conozco, como toda persona que vive en una familia, pero que quiero que me cuentes bien en detalle. Fue muy bonito hacer un seguimiento por la vida de mi madre y no solo por su vida, sino también unos años en los que yo no vivía, los 90 madrileños. Fue algo muy, muy emocionante y muy interesante de hacer”, valora de ese proceso de creación.

Por ello define Una familia como una carta de amor a las madres, y a la suya: “A mí lo que me impulsó a escribir fue mirar un poco más hacia atrás y mirar a mi madre. No me interesaba nada mi vida a la hora de ficcionarla, ni tampoco quería hacer una película sobre, qué sé yo, una familia armenia, y miraba a mi alrededor para buscar lo que me podía inspirar, y encontré la inspiración en mi madre”. En esa carta de amor que es Una familia ha entrado, casi como si fuera otro guiño metacinematográfico, su otra familia, la de un puñado de intérpretes, técnicos y productores que son su familia artística que ha ido sumando durante todos estos años, como esa protagonista que interpreta Claudia Traisac en un papel con el que muestra todo su potencial dramático en una interpretación soberbia.

Hay en esta película una forma de abrazar su pasado, uno que podía haberle metido en el cajón de los niños prodigio del que muchos no salen. Pero también de mirar hacia adelante y ponerle un lazo de despedida. Aclara que él nunca ha “rechazado” esa época. Pero que ponerlo en la ficción en ese guiño “tampoco es un abrazo del todo”. Y lo explica. “Yo siempre he sentido una gratitud y mucho orgullo por haber formado parte durante tantos años de una serie como Cuéntame. Y ese mismo sentimiento de orgullo y de amor hacia la oportunidad y hacia el proyecto, convive y ha convivido siempre con la idea de que este cariño al proyecto no significa que yo mire hacia atrás. Yo necesito mirar hacia adelante y cuando hay algo que tiene una sombra tan larga como esto, la única manera de intentar avanzar es mirando en otra dirección”, señala.

Siente que haber puesto ese guiño se siente como “un punto y aparte”. “Este es el proyecto más importante de mi vida, y empieza con este abrazo a una parte de mi vida que todo el mundo conoce. Y después, es un punto y aparte. También es un punto de inflexión, porque quiero tanto esa parte de mi vida que la utilizo como trampolín del que es el proyecto más importante de mi vida”, zanja. Por ello cree que esta será “la última vez, la última promoción en la que esto tenga cabida”. En todas las entrevistas de todos los proyectos que ha hecho siempre entra esa última pregunta que empieza con esa coletilla: ¿cómo es crecer durante tantos años en la pantalla?

“Tengo 32 años, ya lo he contado mil veces. La gente puede preguntar, pero también para mí, me siento en un momento a nivel artístico, a nivel creativo, en el que me apetece mucho mirar hacia todas las cosas que tengo por delante, y creo que ya no le debo rendir cuentas a nadie para hablar de algo de lo que ya he hablado tantas veces y que nunca he eludido”, apunta

Eso sí, en esa tranquilidad tiene claro que entrarán sus dos facetas. No va a dejar de ser actor, pero esto “no es un capricho” y amenaza con que dirigirá de nuevo, y será “más pronto que tarde”.

Scientists Studied 865 Humpback Whales and Found They Grow Faster Than Expected

📅 Oct 24, 2026  |  Category: Health  |  Read Source →

Despite decades of environmental change and population recovery, eastern Australian humpback whales appear to have maintained their historical adult size.

Eastern Australian humpback whales grow at an extraordinary pace, reaching approximately 10.9 meters (35.8 feet) in length by age five. By six or seven years old, they have already attained about 90 percent of their eventual adult body length, according to new research led by Griffith University.

The findings, published in Marine Mammal Science, suggest these whales grow faster than humpback populations studied elsewhere. “This is faster growth than other humpback whale populations that have been measured,” said lead researcher Stephanie Stack, a Griffith University PhD candidate.

To investigate how humpbacks develop throughout their lives, researchers measured 865 individually identified whales between 2021 and 2024. Using drones to photograph the animals from above, they calculated body lengths without physically disturbing the whales. Long-term photo-identification records helped the team track growth across newborn calves, juveniles, and adults more than 40 years old.

The measurements showed that humpbacks experience their fastest growth during early life, with the rate gradually declining as they approach adult size. Including calves and young whales allowed scientists to examine these crucial developmental stages directly rather than relying on estimates derived from older animals.

“These are also the first contemporary growth curves developed for eastern Australian humpback whales using measurements from living animals, updating knowledge that has relied heavily on measurements collected during the commercial whaling era in the mid-20th century,” Stack said.

Eastern Australia’s humpback population has recovered dramatically since commercial whaling ended, becoming one of the world’s most successful examples of whale conservation. Despite this population rebound and decades of environmental change, the new measurements closely match historical estimates of adult humpback size in Australian waters.

“These new growth curves establish a baseline for investigating how whale biology may respond to changing environmental conditions.”

“Understanding how quickly whales grow provides another piece of the puzzle in understanding the biology of this recovering population,” Stack said. “Growth influences many aspects of a whale’s life, including its health, energy requirements and potentially the timing of reproduction.”

The new growth curves provide researchers with a reference point for tracking possible changes in humpback development as ocean conditions shift. Future measurements could help determine whether environmental changes are affecting growth patterns in this recovering population.

“As environmental conditions continue to change, having robust biological benchmarks like these will be essential for understanding how whale populations are responding,” Stack said.

Reference: “Contemporary Growth Curves for Individual Humpback Whales in Eastern Australia Using Aerial Photogrammetry” by S. H. Stack, T. Fettermann, B. McGovern and S. M. Bengtson Nash, 15 October 2026, Marine Mammal Science. DOI: 10.1111/mms.70296

Early voting begins in New York: What to know and the key races to watch

📅 Oct 24, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Gov. Kathy Hochul and U.S. Rep. Mike Lawler are hoping to keep their seats.

Saturday is the first day of early voting in New York and Empire State voters could help decide which party will control the U.S. House of Representatives over the next two years.

Republicans are seeking to fend off attempts by Democrats to flip congressional seats in the suburbs north of New York City and in the Adirondack Mountains near the Canadian border.

New Yorkers will also decide whether Democrats Kathy Hochul and Letitia James should each be elected to an additional term in their respective roles as governor and attorney general.

Hochul became the state's first female governor in 2021 after then-Gov. Andrew Cuomo resigned amid sexual harassment allegations -- accusations he denied. She was New York's lieutenant governor at the time.

Unlike most New York governors, Hochul is not from New York City or its suburbs, but hails from the Buffalo area -- a more than six-hour drive away. A moderate Democrat, she was largely unknown to voters in the metropolitan area, where much of the state's population resides, until Cuomo's resignation. She defeated Republican Lee Zeldin in 2022, winning a full term in Albany.

In the time since, Hochul has spent a significant amount of time downstate, recently collaborating with current democratic socialist New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani on initiatives like expanded childcare access. Mamdani campaigned with Hochul on Friday and has released videos on social media in support of her campaign.

Hochul's Republican opponent in 2026, Nassau County Executive Bruce Blakeman, has worked to tie Hochul to Mamdani throughout his campaign, issuing a statement at one point that referenced the "Hochul-Mamdani agenda."

Blakeman has centered his gubernatorial campaign on fighting crime and lowering taxes in New York State. He has received President Donald Trump's endorsement and been adamant in his support of the president, even campaigning with him at a rally in Syracuse in early October.

A recent Quinnipiac University poll of likely New York State voters found that 55% would support Hochul and 41% would Blakeman.

In the attorney general's race, incumbent Letitia James is facing a challenge from Republican former Drug Enforcement Administration Chief of Staff Saritha Komatireddy. James gained national attention through her 2022 lawsuit against Trump, his company and members of his family alleging net worth inflation. The lawsuit led to a $454 million civil fraud judgment that was thrown out by an appeals court in 2025.

While Komatireddy has not historically been a household name in the state, many of her campaign materials using the "just fight crime" branding have gained significant attention on social media.

One of the most high-profile races in the country is in New York's 17th Congressional District in the lower Hudson Valley, where incumbent Republican U.S. Rep. Mike Lawler is being challenged by Democrat Cait Conley.

The district is home to everyone from former President Bill Clinton to New York City investment bankers to thousands of police officers.

Trump attended a rally with Lawler in the district in May while former U.S. Secretary of Transportation Pete Buttigieg came to the district in September to campaign with Conley.

The race in the 17th Congressional District is not the only potentially competitive race within a stone's throw of Manhattan. In the 3rd Congressional District, which straddles Nassau and Queens, incumbent Democratic U.S. Rep. Tom Suozzi is being challenged by Republican Michael LiPetri, a former state assemblyman who has run for Congress twice before.

In New York's 21st Congressional District, which stretches from the historically industrial Mohawk Valley along the Erie Canal to the Canadian border, Democrats are hoping that 2026 is their best chance to flip the seat currently held by Republican U.S. Rep. Elise Stefanik.

Stefanik previously planned to seek the Republican gubernatorial nomination, but ended up dropping out of the governor's race and announcing she would not seek reelection to the House.

Republican Anthony Constantino, a sticker company CEO endorsed by Trump, is facing off against Democratic farmer Blake Gendebien in the district. Trump endorsed Constantino in the Republican primary over a state lawmaker who had been endorsed by the head of New York State's Republican Party.

Tariffs and American relations with Canada have been top of mind in parts of the district.

Early voting concludes on Nov. 1. Election Day is Nov. 3.

NY archdiocese finalizing $800M settlement with clergy sex abuse survivors...

📅 Oct 24, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Agreement with more than 1,300 victims of clergy members and lay leaders would resolve long legal fight

The Catholic archdiocese of New York is finalizing a settlement for at least $800m with more than 1,300 people who said they were sexually abused by clergy members and lay leaders, according to one of the plaintiff’s attorneys.

The agreement would resolve a long legal fight, as well as reportedly reduce the risk of the diocese going bankrupt.

While the parties have not reached a final deal, just a few details remain, according to attorney Paul Mones, who represents some of the plaintiffs.

“It gives closure to people, many of whom are in their 60s, 70s and even into their 80s,” Mones said in a phone interview on Saturday. “That is a significant matter in terms of closing the emotional door – or trying to – because it’s never going to be closed, but [it] at least gives some measure of justice to people who were terribly wronged.”

In 2006, the New York state court of appeals refused to waive a statute of limitations concerning allegations that Catholic priests committed sexual abuse in the 1960s, 1970s and 1980s, the New York Times reported.

The state then in 2019 passed the Child Victims Act, which allowed sexual abuse survivors to bring civil lawsuits against abusers until the plaintiff turned 55. Afterwards, thousands of people filed lawsuits against the archdiocese, the Boy Scouts and Jeffrey Epstein, among others.

Then, in 2025, the archdiocese of New York started trying to raise $300m to pay 1,300 survivors of clergy abuse. The New York Roman Catholic district sold off assets and cut costs to settle the claims; in New Orleans, the archdiocese declared bankruptcy amid similar scandals.

The New York survivors can now either enter into an agreement with the archdiocese in which each receives $250,000, or fill out a form detailing what happened and participate in an allocation procedure that will determine how much money they receive, Mones explained.

In an email to priests, which was shared with the New York Times, the New York archbishop, Ronald A Hicks, said: “We are on the verge of being able to, please God, bring some measure of healing and reconciliation to all victim-survivors.”

While the settlement would resolve the claims in New York, the Catholic church faces lawsuits concerning alleged sexual abuse in other states.

Chicago attorneys filed a half dozen lawsuits this month against the city’s archdiocese alleging that the institution knew its priests were running a sex ring at a parish and did not stop it, according to WTTW News. In Baltimore, the archdiocese is in mediation with sexual abuse survivors, WBAL reported.

Mones said that he is representing alleged survivors of sexual abuse in lawsuits against archdioceses across the country, including Buffalo, San Diego and San Francisco.

Healey’s first budget to include big devolution package to boost growth

📅 Oct 24, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Chancellor will build on Burnham’s promise that local leaders in England will no longer have to ‘come cap in hand to Westminster’

Andy Burnham has promised that local leaders in England will no longer have to come “cap in hand to Westminster” ahead of a fresh drive to devolve spending power in next week’s budget.

In his statement on Wednesday, the chancellor, John Healey, will include plans for a new £1.5bn borrowing facility, to be drawn up in collaboration with mayors over the next two years.

Regional mayors have long complained that they are constrained by their limited borrowing power – in contrast to local authorities in the US and some European countries, which commonly issue their own bonds.

The borrowing plan will form part of a big devolution package aimed at boosting regional economic growth. This will also include the allocation of an initial £285m “good growth” funding next year, to be spent on local leaders’ priorities – which could include housing, transport links or high street regeneration.

Amid fears in the Treasury about whether some local authorities have the capacity to administer increasingly large budgets, Healey will also announce £150m of support to help them scale up and build expertise.

Hailing the plans, Burnham said they would mean local leaders would no longer need to come to the Treasury for approval for each individual project. “I’m calling time on local leaders going cap in hand to Westminster. That is no way to run a country. When I became prime minister, I said that if a decision can be made closer to home, it should be.

“Today we’re putting that into practice, shifting power out of Westminster and into the hands of the people who know their communities best.”

The £285m in “good growth” funding is intended to tide local authorities over until a new system comes into force in 2028 whereby some income tax revenue will be handed directly to their areas to give them longer-term security – an idea first mooted by Healey’s predecessor, Rachel Reeves, earlier this year.

Physicists Use Light To Unlock a Crystal’s Hidden Electrical Powers

📅 Oct 24, 2026  |  Category: Science  |  Read Source →

A crystal’s laser-induced electrical order lasts just a trillionth of a second, giving researchers a way to explore ultrafast approaches to processing and storing information.

Inside a crystal, tiny electrical regions can point in different directions or line up in an organized pattern. Cooling helps bring them into alignment, but physicists at Auburn University have now shown that brief flashes of laser light can also produce order at warmer temperatures, where those regions would otherwise remain disorganized.

The team worked with single crystals of a modified form of strontium titanate. The material allows electrons to move through it while also containing tiny regions with distinct positive and negative sides, an unusual combination of electrical properties. These regions are known as polar nanoregions.

For decades, physicists have debated whether atoms shift together to create the material’s polarized state or whether its polar regions already exist and become organized as it cools. Telling those explanations apart has been difficult because the regions are so small and their signals so faint.

Wencan Jin, the Thomas and Jean Walter Endowed Associate Professor in Auburn’s Department of Physics, led the research with graduate student Mohamed Kandil. The project began at the 2023 American Physical Society March Meeting, where Jin connected with researchers in Japan who could grow the crystals. Collaborators at Japan’s National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology grew the samples used in the experiments.

The Auburn researchers built a highly sensitive laser-based measurement system, then spent nearly a year improving the equipment before detecting the regions’ faint optical signal. By measuring how the crystal changed incoming light, they could track the regions’ alignment through repeated rounds of warming and cooling. The measurements showed disordered directions at higher temperatures giving way to preferred orientations as the crystal cooled. This provided strong evidence that the polar regions were already present before they lined up.

The team still had to capture the fleeting moment when laser illumination brought the regions into alignment. “The breakthrough came in 2025, when Mohamed finally observed the disordered polar regions becoming aligned under laser illumination,” Jin said. “Seeing that signal emerge was a thrilling moment for the team.”

The light-induced alignment lasted only a picosecond, or one trillionth of a second (10⁻¹² second), but an optical measurement could still detect the resulting pattern. Being able to create and read that brief arrangement with light gives researchers a way to investigate extremely fast approaches to processing and storing information. It also provides a tool for studying electrical behavior that is otherwise difficult to observe.

“This experiment has truly been Mohamed’s odyssey,” Jin said. “It took him three years of dedication to complete the work and achieve his first publication.”
