Taxi Strömsund nobbar färjan – kör skolskjuts via omledningsvägen

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Taxi Strömsund väljer fortsatt omledningsvägen för skolskjuts i Strömsund efter avbrott med färjetrafiken. Detta trots att vägen är betydligt mindre tidseffektiv än färjetrafiken.

– Vi behöver kontinuitet i trafiken, säger Douglas Forsberg, ägare.

Sedan färjetrafiken i Strömsund startat har färjan varit avstängd vid tre tillfällen. Något som har åtgärdats av Trafikverket, men fått Taxi Strömsund, som sköter skolskjutsarna i kommunen, att avvakta.

– Vi har tillsammans med Länstrafiken och Strömsunds kommun tagit beslutet att fortsätta köra skolbarnen längs omledningsvägen två veckor till minst, säger Linnea Persson, trafikansvarig på Taxi Strömsund.

Företaget har sedan broskadan varit drabbade av förseningar och resursbrist. Linnea beskriver det som dag och natt från tiden innan, och att de idag har svårt att hinna med alla körningar.

Men trots att företaget kämpar med tidtabeller och omledningsvägen är mindre tidseffektiv än färjan känner verksamheten att det är den bästa lösningen – eftersom de inte tycker att färjetrafiken flyter på så bra som de hade önskat.

– Den är inte stabil, säger Douglas Forsberg, ägare.

Normalt är det 59 barn som är i behov av skolskjuts i Strömsund, men efter broskadan har Taxi Strömsund idag 129 elever att köra.

• Strömsundsbron är helt avstängd efter att ha skadats i samband med en tung transport sent på kvällen tisdagen 29 september

• En lastbil på cirka 100 ton hade fått dispens av Trafikverket för att köra över bron, trots att brons bärighetsklass tillåter fordon som väger upp till 74 ton. I samband med transporten reste sig bron omkring 40 centimeter på norra sidan

• Ingen person skadades i samband med händelsen

• Lastbilen stod kvar på bron i nästan 16 dygn, men kunde köras av bron 15 oktober

• Under tiden bron är avstängd leds trafiken om såhär: Välj väg 795 från Hallviken mot väg 345 mot Ulriksfors och vidare mot Strömsund och E45 för norrgående trafik och tvärtom för södergående. Under onsdag 7 oktober skyltade Trafikverket upp ytterligare en omledningsväg för trafik som inte ska till Strömsund

• Från 22 oktober och framåt trafikerar Trafikverket Ströms vattudal med två färjor, det finns en prioritetsordning för vilka som får åka med färjorna

• Från och med 30 oktober får gång- och cykeltrafikanter återigen använda bron

• Enligt Trafikverkets prognos ska bron kunna öppnas för fordonstrafik 31 januari 2027

På grund av personalbrist är det begränsad färjetrafik över Ströms vattudal. Färjorna kommer att gå en gång i timmen istället för varje halvtimme från respektive färjeläge, tills problemet är löst.

Trafikverket ska gå igenom alla Strömsundsbrons beståndsdelar, till minsta skruv och nit, och åtgärda de övriga skador som finns utöver det nu bortmonterade pendellagret.

I videon berättar Simon Henriksson på Trafikverket om arbetet.

I samband med pressträffen meddelade Trafikverket att de färjor som just nu trafikerar Ströms vattudal kommer att fortsätta att gå enligt tidtabell. Det blir två testveckor nu och sedan kommer färjetrafiken att utvärderas.

– Jag vill betona att det är en prognos, säger Theresa Gellerstedt avdelningschef Trafikverket.

Planen är att bron ska kunna stabiliseras med hjälp av spännstag. Dessa spännstag ska ersätta det trasiga pendellagret. Arbete kommer parallellt att göras på bron. Enligt Trafikverket kommer sannolikt nedsatt hastighet att råda på bron.

Trafikverket: Strömsundsbron öppnar för gång och cykeltrafik i morgon fredag.

Det meddelar Theresa Gellerstedt avdelningschef Trafikverket på myndighetens presskonferens.

Max 100 personer får vistas på bron samtidigt, men Trafikverket kommer inte att kunna kontrollera så att det efterlevs. När snösäsongen drar i gång kommer Trafikverket att sköta halk- och snöröjning för fotgängare och cyklister.

Theresa Gellerstedt säger också att Trafikverket har förståelse för att situationen är tuff för invånarna i Strömsund och att myndigheten därför prioriterat arbete med att kunna öppna för gång- och cykeltrafikanter.

Trafikverket ska lämna besked om Strömsundsbron i dag

Besked om prognos för hur länge Strömsundsbron på E45 ska vara avstängd väntas i dag. Klockan 10:30 håller Trafikverket presskonferens under rubriken: Då återupptas trafiken på Strömsundsbron

Scott Gillingham re-elected as mayor of Winnipeg

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Scott Gillingham was re-elected as Winnipeg's mayor on Wednesday.JOHN WOODS/The Canadian Press

Scott Gillingham was re-elected as the mayor of Winnipeg on Wednesday after a campaign largely dominated by issues ranging from housing to public safety.

Unofficial results showed Gillingham received 80,206 votes to send him to a second term, while former legislature member and city councillor Kevin Klein drew 41,703 votes.

Software developer Noah Redden came in third place with 39,962 votes, and funeral home director Mike Vogiatzakis received 19,463.

Supporters cheered as Gillingham claimed victory late Wednesday.

“Tonight you have given me the honour of serving as your mayor once again,” he told those gathered at a watch party. “Thank you, Winnipeg. I will never take your trust for granted.”

Gillingham said the city’s best days are still ahead. He’s vowed to find solutions to the multitude of issues plaguing the city, such as homelessness, addictions, mental health and crime.

There are many reasons to be optimistic about Winnipeg, Gillingham said, noting new home builds and infrastructure, and revitalizing the city’s downtown.

He said he wants to build on that momentum over the next four years.

“So whether you voted for me or not, I want you to know that I will be your mayor too. My door will be open, your voice will matter and I’ll work every day to make this city better for your family and my family,” he said.

Klein, who owns newspapers including the Winnipeg Sun, congratulated Gillingham in a concession speech at the Norwood Hotel.

He said he was humbled by the support he received and that there would be a news conference Friday to wrap up his campaign and announce his next steps.

Klein previously had an unsuccessful run at the city’s top job in 2022.

Almost all other Manitoba communities also held elections Wednesday, except for the resort areas of Winnipeg Beach, Dunnottar and Victoria Beach, which held votes in July.

A fire broke out at a major nuclear research complex in South Carolina that supports the US nuclear weapons program, the Department of Energy has said.

“No contamination has been reported outside the affected facility,” the department said in a statement.

Emergency protocols were activated, with employees not involved in response efforts ordered to suspend operations and shelter in place as a precaution. The site was closed to incoming traffic, while nearby rail, river, and air traffic was restricted.

Officials lifted the emergency declaration at 9:45 PM, saying radiation surveys found no detectable contamination. Protective measures were lifted, and the site was cleared to resume normal operations on Thursday.

Local media reported that the fire broke out in a single room and involved an unfiltered, ground-level radiological release. One employee was reportedly hospitalized with minor injuries.

Established in the early 1950s during the Cold War, the SRS originally produced plutonium and tritium for US nuclear weapons. Although its production reactors have since been shut down, the complex continues to play a key role in maintaining the American nuclear arsenal.

The SRS processes and replenishes tritium, a radioactive isotope of hydrogen used to boost the explosive yield of nuclear warheads. It also manages nuclear materials, treats radioactive waste, and carries out environmental cleanup.

The US government is also developing a facility at the SRS to manufacture plutonium cores for nuclear warheads as part of efforts to modernize the country’s nuclear weapons stockpile.

The incident comes less than two weeks after a fire swept through Canadian Forces Base Wainwright in Alberta, killing four soldiers and injuring 39 others during a live-fire training exercise. According to the Canadian military, strong winds caused the fire to spread rapidly, overtaking an area occupied by troops.

The Canadian Armed Forces said last week that 27 personnel remained hospitalized, while 12 were discharged. Military police have launched an independent investigation into the incident.

US hospitals and nursing homes are breeding grounds for deadly superbugs – CDC

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Health  |  Read Source →

More than four in five US deaths linked to drug-resistant infections involve pathogens commonly associated with hospitals and nursing homes, according to a new report from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).

The agency’s Antimicrobial Resistance Threats Report released this week draws on data from 2023 and estimates that 19 types of drug-resistant bacteria and fungi cause 9.7 million infections and 50,800 deaths annually in the US. Seven bacterial threats commonly associated with hospitals and nursing homes accounted for more than 42,000 of those deaths, along with nearly $12 billion in healthcare costs.

Antimicrobial resistance occurs when germs develop the ability to withstand drugs designed to kill them, making infections difficult or impossible to treat. Elderly people, those with weakened immune systems, and patients undergoing invasive medical procedures are particularly vulnerable.

Among the pathogens classified as urgent threats is Candida auris, a potentially deadly fungus that can resist multiple medications and survive on surfaces in healthcare facilities. It primarily affects seriously ill patients and can cause dangerous bloodstream infections.

A separate CDC study published in July found that reported cases of Candida auris more than doubled from 2022-2024, rising from 2,882 to 6,197. Most cases for which the type of medical facility was identified were detected in hospitals, including those that provide long-term treatment for seriously ill patients.

The CDC noted that many healthcare-associated infections are preventable, but much of the progress in combating drug resistance was lost during the Covid-19 pandemic, when staffing shortages, overcrowding, and disruptions to infection-control practices contributed to a rise in infections.

The warning comes as US hospitals face mounting financial and staffing pressures. A Harvard-led study published earlier this month found that 432 hospitals closed nationwide from 2010-2025, resulting in a net loss of 216 facilities and more than 24,000 beds.

Nursing homes are also struggling with bed and staffing shortages. An investigation in September by KFF Health News found that declining capacity forced some patients to remain hospitalized longer because suitable care facilities were unavailable.

Russia and Tanzania have agreed to launch a joint space program, the Russian space agency Roscosmos has said. Moscow will assist Dodoma in establishing its own national space agency and developing the infrastructure needed for a domestic space industry.

On Wednesday, Roscosmos and Tanzania’s Ministry of Information, Communication and Information Technology signed a framework memorandum on strategic partnership and cooperation on the sidelines of the Russia-Africa summit. The event has brought delegations from 48 African countries to Moscow this week.

“Tanzania is planning to establish its own space agency, and we are ready to provide our colleagues with expert and methodological support,” Roscosmos Director General Dmitry Bakanov told journalists. The Russian space agency has already stepped up cooperation with Tanzanian specialists in the field, with support from the Russian presidential administration, he added.

According to Bakanov, Dodoma is particularly interested in developing its own spacecraft and launching them into orbit aboard Russian rockets. Roscosmos has confirmed that Russia will launch Tanzania’s first Earth remote-sensing satellite in March 2027.

During the summit, Russian specialists demonstrated to the Tanzanian delegation the country’s capabilities in training cosmonauts and space industry specialists, as well as conducting scientific experiments in space.

The two sides also discussed sharing Earth remote-sensing data and establishing ground stations in Tanzania to receive such data. According to Bakanov, the African nation also plans to increase the number of students studying space-related disciplines at Russian universities.

Under the agreement, Russia will assist Tanzania in developing infrastructure for its space industry, while the two countries will exchange expertise and best practices in training space specialists. They have also agreed to establish joint working groups and develop road maps covering all areas of cooperation.

Moscow has been actively supporting African nations’ space ambitions. Last year, the South African National Space Agency (SANSA) hailed Russia as a key partner in advancing its national space program. Pretoria “has received most of its support from the Russian Federation,” SANSA board chairman Patrick Ndlovu told Sputnik Africa at the time. Earlier that year, SANSA CEO Humbulani Mudau announced South Africa’s support for Russia’s proposal to establish a BRICS Space Council to strengthen space cooperation among member states.

In 2024, Zimbabwe announced plans to build its first spaceport with Russian assistance. The country also said it was in talks with Roscosmos about gaining access to Russia’s cosmonaut training system, among other areas of cooperation.
